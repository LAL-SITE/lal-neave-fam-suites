## Built from the firm's filed exemplar (Motion for Order to Conduct Trial via Zoom)

## STEP 0 — IS A MOTION EVEN NEEDED?
Check first, in order: (1) the trial/hearing order — format may already be remote
or hybrid; (2) divisional standing Zoom procedures (lal-judicial-procedures — ask
before web search) — some divisions allow remote appearance by stipulation or JA
email; (3) OC stipulation — a joint stipulation or agreed order is faster and
cheaper than a contested motion. Only draft the motion when the order requires
in-person and no cheaper path exists. **Authority framework:** communication
technology appearances are governed by Fla. R. Gen. Prac. & Jud. Admin. 2.530 and
Rule 12.451 — testimony by remote means generally requires court authorization
(stipulation or motion/order); flag any oath-administration logistics for remote
witnesses.

## STEP 1 — SCOPE THE REQUEST (who + what proceeding)
Define precisely: counsel only / a party / specific witnesses / all participants;
which proceeding (identify by date and setting order). Overbroad requests draw
denials — the firm's filed exemplar requested all parties, counsel, and witnesses for
one identified trial date and stated why for each group.

## STEP 2 — DRAFT (firm exemplar structure)
Caption per lal-pleading-standard. Title: **MOTION FOR AN ORDER TO CONDUCT
[TRIAL/HEARING] ON [DATE] VIA ZOOM** (or "...FOR REMOTE APPEARANCE OF [NAME]").
COMES NOW opening; numbered grounds per the filed exemplar pattern:
1. Who requests remote appearance and for what proceeding;
2. Concrete hardship — travel distance with specifics (party/witness locations by
   city and state), cost, work/childcare impracticability;
3. Judicial efficiency and reduced expense with full participation preserved;
4. **Conferral paragraph (firm-standard candor):** state that conferral occurred,
   by what method, and OC's position — agreed / opposed / no response. Never omit;
   never misstate.
WHEREFORE: (A) grant and permit [scope] to appear via Zoom; (B) further relief.
Certificate of service; firm signature block. Attach proposed order granting the
specific scope (courts sign specific orders, not vague ones).

## STEP 3 — NOTICE OF HEARING + SETTING
If opposed, set for hearing (UMC or motion calendar per division): draft the NOH
per the firm's E-Filing Procedure for Notices of Hearing (Desktop How-To),
coordinate the date with the JA per divisional procedure, serve, and calendar.
**Timing rule: file early.** The firm's exemplar motion was filed ~5 weeks before trial
and heard ~3 weeks out. A Zoom motion inside T-14 risks being unheard — RUSH flag.

## STEP 4 — AFTER RULING
Granted: distribute the order + Zoom logistics to client and witnesses
(lal-correspondence for client instructions), update the tracker (Witnesses tab
Appearance column), confirm the court reporter is on the link
(lal-court-reporter), and test tech at T-3. Denied: update travel plans and
witness subpoena logistics immediately; consider stipulated telephone testimony
for minor witnesses per 2.530.
