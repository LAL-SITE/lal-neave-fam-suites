## PART 1 — POSTURE MAP (pick the lane before doing anything)

| Goal | Lane | Key authority |
|---|---|---|
| Get the case set for trial | Notice for Trial — case must be "at issue" (motions to last pleading disposed of, or 20 days after last pleading) | Rule 12.440; pretrial conf. per 12.200 |
| Try the pending pleadings | Trial-preparation track (this suite) | Trial order controls |
| Force discovery before trial | Discovery enforcement track | 12.380; lal-advanced-discovery |
| Move the trial date | Continuance track — written motion, reason, opposing position, readiness date, client signature except good cause | Rule 12.460; R. Jud. Admin. 2.545(e) |
| Keep evidence out pretrial | Motion in limine — specific evidence, valid basis, narrowly tailored; not a disguised SJ motion; pretrial ruling may still require live objection to preserve | Ch. 90; firm in-limine system |
| Challenge a GM/HO result | Exceptions / motion to vacate — 10-day clock, transcript ordinarily required | Rule 12.490 (GM), 12.491 (support HO) |
| Attack an order after trial | Rehearing/clarification/vacate/appeal tracks — never blended | 12.530, 12.540 |

Never blend lanes in one document. Selecting the vehicle by urgency instead of legal
function is the #1 drafting error the firm systems exist to prevent.

## PART 2 — DEADLINE & SETTING FRAMEWORK

- **Firm SOP:** every trial/hearing order gets same-day forwarding (Jeri), 24-hour
  intake (paralegal), the T-14/T-7/T-3 Deadline Ladder, the standardized
  "YYYY-MM-DD Trial" folder (00–05), {{PM_SYSTEM}} stage = Trial bound / Evidentiary
  hearing scheduled, and weekly trial-first case review.
- **Date math:** R. Jud. Admin. 2.514 governs computation; weekend/holiday rolls;
  ambiguous triggers resolved to the EARLIER date + attorney flag.
- **Order archetypes seen in firm matters:** (a) layered-ladder orders (expert
  disclosure T-60; last day to serve written discovery T-45; discovery close T-10;
  pretrial statement T-10; exhibit exchange T-7; fee affidavits T-48h; joint
  personal property inventory; joint ED worksheet); (b) strict-disclosure orders
  (all trial evidence specifically identified within 30 days; vague or catch-all
  disclosures are nullities; non-compliance = INADMISSIBLE). Always extract from
  the four corners; never assume either archetype.
- **Supplementation:** Rules 12.280(g)/12.285(f) — prior disclosures must be
  supplemented when materially incomplete; current FA required for trial.
- **Mediation:** many orders require completed mediation before trial — check.

## PART 3 — EVIDENCE CODE MAP (Ch. 90, firm statute file)

**Relevance spine:** 90.401 (definition) → 90.402 (relevant evidence admissible) →
90.403 (exclusion: probative value substantially outweighed by unfair prejudice,
confusion, cumulativeness) → 90.404/90.405 (character; specific instances limits) →
90.406 (routine practice) → 90.408 (compromise offers inadmissible to prove
liability — mediation/settlement talk stays out) → 90.410 (plea offers).

**Privileges (90.501–90.510):** lawyer-client (90.502); psychotherapist-patient
(90.503) — HUGE in family cases (therapy records, evaluations); husband-wife
(90.504); clergy (90.505); domestic violence advocate-victim (90.5036); sexual
assault counselor (90.5035). Waiver traps: a party who puts their mental health at
issue may open the door — attorney call, flag it.

**Witnesses (90.601–90.616):** competency presumed (90.601); oath (90.605);
personal knowledge required (90.604); judge/juror can't testify (90.607);
impeachment — who may impeach (90.608: bias, prior inconsistent statement, defect
of capacity, character for untruthfulness, contradiction); character evidence limits
(90.609); prior convictions (90.610: felony or dishonesty crimes); leading
questions/mode of interrogation (90.612 — leading OK on cross and for adverse
parties); refreshing recollection with writings (90.613 context / 90.953 originals);
prior statements of witnesses (90.614); calling witnesses by court (90.615);
**sequestration — invoke "the Rule" (90.616)**: witnesses excluded on request;
parties exempt.

**Opinion (90.701–90.706):** lay opinion limited to perception-based (90.701);
expert opinion under 90.702 (Daubert standard codified — sufficient facts, reliable
principles, reliably applied); basis may include inadmissible facts if reasonably
relied upon (90.704); disclosure of basis on cross (90.705).

**Hearsay (90.801–90.806):** definition + non-hearsay (90.801 — admissions of a
party-opponent are NOT hearsay: the other side's texts/emails/statements come in
against them; prior inconsistent statements under oath); the rule (90.802);
**key 90.803 exceptions for family trials** (availability immaterial):
(1) spontaneous statement, (2) excited utterance, (3) then-existing mental/emotional/
physical condition (child's state of mind), (4) medical diagnosis/treatment
statements, (5) recorded recollection, **(6) business records** (see predicates),
(8) public records, (23) child victim hearsay (strict procedural findings required —
attorney-level issue). 90.804 (declarant unavailable): former testimony, statement
against interest. 90.805 hearsay-within-hearsay: EVERY layer needs an exception
(text message quoting grandma = 2 layers). 90.806: attacking declarant credibility.

**Authentication (90.901–90.902):** authentication by evidence sufficient to
support a finding (90.901 — testimony of knowledge, distinctive characteristics,
reply doctrine); self-authentication (90.902 — certified public records, official
publications, **(11) certified business records** in lieu of custodian testimony).

**Originals/best evidence (90.951–90.958):** duplicates generally admissible
(90.953); admissibility of other evidence of contents (90.954).

**Judicial notice (90.201–90.207 + Rule 12.4501):** mandatory (laws, 90.201) vs.
discretionary (court records, facts not subject to dispute, 90.202); request +
notice procedure (90.203–90.204); family-case emergency judicial notice of related
case records (Rule 12.4501, 2-business-day notice filing).

## PART 4 — PREDICATE PLAYBOOK (the questions that get it in)

**Business records (90.803(6) + 90.902(11) + § 92.605 out-of-state records):**
Either a records custodian testifies OR a certification/declaration is served in
advance. Elements: (1) made at or near the time of the event; (2) by or from a
person with knowledge; (3) kept in the course of a regularly conducted business
activity; (4) regular practice to make it. Bank statements, payroll records,
school records, medical bills all ride this rail. PLAN AHEAD: certifications must
be obtained and noticed before trial — this is a subpoena-workflow task, not a
trial-day fix.

**Text messages / social media / emails:** two hurdles, always separately:
(1) AUTHENTICATION (90.901) — testimony of a participant, distinctive
characteristics (number/handle, context, reply thread), device forensics; and
(2) HEARSAY — opposing party's messages = party admission (90.801, not hearsay);
client's own messages offered by us = hearsay problem unless a real exception or
non-truth purpose (effect on listener, notice). Screenshots: best-evidence issues
are usually resolved by duplicate rule (90.953) but be ready to show completeness.

**Photographs / video:** witness with knowledge testifies it fairly and accurately
depicts the scene/event (90.901). Date/source questions go to weight — but strict
disclosure orders require the specific item to be disclosed.

**Financial affidavits / discovery responses of the other side:** party admissions
— not hearsay when offered against the maker; certified filings self-authenticate.

**Court records from other cases (criminal disposition, injunction, DCF):**
certified copies (90.902) + judicial notice (90.202(6)) where appropriate; watch
90.610 limits when the point is impeachment by conviction.

**Composite financial exhibits (firm practice):** the firm builds composite income
exhibits + charts (per filed exemplars) — the composite is a demonstrative/summary;
underlying records must each be independently admissible and available. Disclose
the composite itself as an exhibit.

## PART 5 — OBJECTION QUICK TABLE (spot it both ways)

| Objection | Rule | Fast response when we offer |
|---|---|---|
| Hearsay | 90.802 | Party admission (90.801); 90.803 exception by element; non-truth purpose |
| Lack of foundation/predicate | 90.901/90.604 | Lay the predicate (Part 4); tender the certification |
| Relevance | 90.401/402 | Tie to a pled issue / statutory factor (61.13(3), 61.08, 61.075, 61.30) |
| Unfair prejudice / cumulative | 90.403 | Probative value argument; offer to limit scope |
| Improper character | 90.404 | Character in issue (parenting capacity), habit (90.406), impeachment rail |
| Leading | 90.612 | Cross/adverse witness — leading is proper |
| Speculation / improper lay opinion | 90.701 | Perception-based; or qualify the expert (90.702) |
| Privilege | 90.502/503/504 | Waiver/at-issue analysis — attorney only |
| Best evidence | 90.952–954 | Duplicate admissible (90.953) |
| Beyond scope of disclosure | Trial order | Only defense is compliance — this is why the intake skill exists |

Preservation: get a RULING on the record; make a proffer when excluded (12.470 —
no formal "exception" needed after a considered objection).

## PART 6 — WITNESS RULES THAT BITE

- **Children:** Rule 12.407 — no deposition, subpoena, or attendance without prior
  order on good cause; applies to remote proceedings; ensure children cannot
  overhear Zoom hearings.
- **Remote testimony:** R. Jud. Admin. 2.530 + Rule 12.451 — motion/stipulation
  required; out-of-area witnesses need advance arrangements and the party calling
  them provides the link/tech.
- **Subpoenas:** Rule 12.410 — trial subpoenas and subpoenas duces tecum; serve
  early; witness fee + mileage; records subpoenas coordinate with § 92.605 /
  90.902(11) certifications. Track service in the tracker.
- **Sequestration:** invoke 90.616 at the start; instruct our witnesses not to
  discuss testimony.
- **Experts:** 90.702 (Daubert); disclosure per trial order (often T-60 with area +
  subject matter); fees/CV/prior testimony ready for voir dire.
- **The client:** prep is a workflow, not a conversation — direct outline from the
  statutory factors; cross-prep from the other side's disputed-issues list; exhibits
  the client must authenticate rehearsed one by one.

## PART 7 — GM / HEARING OFFICER LANE (Rule 12.490 / 12.491)

Referral requires no objection within 10 days (support HO matters differ — check
12.491); objection grounds include credibility-heavy disputes and relief beyond
authority. GM hearings ARE evidentiary hearings — full evidence rules apply.
Proceedings are often only electronically recorded: ARRANGE A COURT REPORTER —
review of the recommended order (exceptions/motion to vacate, 10 days from service)
ordinarily fails without a transcript. Recommended order → order adopting;
post-adoption attacks use the proper vacate/rehearing lane. Firm exemplars: Motion
for Referral to GM, Orders of Referral (GM & HO), Exception to GM Report (PLEADINGS
EXAMPLES folder in the training packet).

## PART 8 — TRIAL NOTEBOOK & TRIAL-DAY STANDARDS (firm operating system)

Notebook: (1) trial order + deadline ladder; (2) pleadings being heard; (3) opening
outline / theory of the case; (4) witness order + direct outlines + cross outlines;
(5) exhibit binder w/ tracker (offered/admitted columns); (6) objection cheat rows
(Part 5); (7) financial worksheets (CS guidelines, ED, arrears, fee affidavits);
(8) proposed final judgment / proposed orders; (9) logistics (JA contact, Zoom
link, reporter confirmation, interpreter, client instructions).
Trial-day: exhibits pre-marked per the court's practice; bench copies; redaction
verified (2.420/2.425); tech tested for remote; client prepped on demeanor;
stipulations confirmed on the record; proposed FJ ready in editable Word.
Post-trial: order review module — deadlines for rehearing (12.530) run from
rendition; calendar immediately on receipt.
