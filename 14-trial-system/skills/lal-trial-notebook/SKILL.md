## TAB STRUCTURE (build in this order)

**Tab 1 — Control.** Trial/hearing order + Intake Memo + Deadline Ladder (all
items shown satisfied or flagged); judge procedures summary; command center
Trial/Evidentiary Deadline Tracker snapshot.
**Tab 2 — Pleadings being tried.** The operative petition/counterpetition/answer
and any pending motions to be heard — from folder 00; verify the pleadings match
what the order says is being heard.
**Tab 3 — Theory & openings.** Theory of the case (one sentence from the Issue
Organizer header); opening outline (attorney's); the Trial Issue Organizer
(Word/HTML export) — the backbone document.
**Tab 4 — Witnesses.** One section per witness IN CALL ORDER (attorney sets):
witness prep packet (lal-witness-prep output: direct outline / cross outline),
subpoena + proof of service, expected-testimony statement from the filed list,
prior statements/deposition excerpts for impeachment or refreshing.
**Tab 5 — Exhibits.** The filed exhibit lists (both sides); the Exhibit Tracker
(current export — foundation, objection, exchanged, pre-mark columns complete);
the exhibits themselves in list order, pre-marked per the court's practice, with
the court's exhibit-grid form if the division uses one; § 90.956 notices filed
for summaries; redaction verified (2.420/2.425).
**Tab 6 — Objections.** The two-sided Objection Review Chart; the objection
quick-table (rule + fast response); rulings from motions in limine with
"renew live to preserve" reminders; proffer plan for anything excluded (12.470).
**Tab 7 — Numbers.** CS guidelines worksheet, ED schedule/equalizer, composite
income exhibits with their working (source-tagged) versions, fee affidavit +
fee summary chart, arrears calculations — every figure Russ-verified against
source.
**Tab 8 — Proposed relief.** Proposed FJ / proposed orders (editable Word copies
noted as available for the JA), proposed parenting plan, any partial
stipulations signed.
**Tab 9 — Logistics.** Courtroom/Zoom details, JA contact, court reporter
confirmation (T-1 reconfirm noted), interpreter, witness appearance schedule
with contact numbers, client instructions (dress, arrival, demeanor — sent via
lal-correspondence), spellings sheet for the reporter, copies count
(bench + witness + OC), tech check note for remote.
**Tab 10 — Law.** Verified caselaw from the project file (each with its one-line
holding note and attorney-verification status), statutory excerpts for the
factors in play (61.13(3)/61.30/61.075/61.08/61.16 as applicable), the
trial-hearing reference posture map. NOTHING in this tab that is not
project-file verified (lal-caselaw-protocol).

## THE READINESS GAP CHECK (produced by every build)
As each tab assembles, score it: COMPLETE / GAP (missing item, owner, fix-by
date) / FLAG (attorney decision needed). Output the one-page **Trial Readiness
Report** to Marta Estevez and the attorney:
```
TRIAL READINESS — [Matter] — [Trial date] — Build: [T-14 / T-3]
Tab | Status | Gaps (item — owner — fix-by) | Flags
...
FILING/EXCHANGE CHECKLIST (from Intake Memo §5): each item ✔/✘
VERDICT: READY / READY WITH GAPS / NOT READY — [what changes that]
```
The T-3 build must be READY or the attorney is deciding about continuance
(lal-continuance-motion) with facts, not hope.
