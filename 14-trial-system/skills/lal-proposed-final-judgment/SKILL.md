# Neave Family Law Group, P.A. Proposed Final Judgment Pipeline
## Paralegal assembles | Russ verifies every number | Marta Estevez reviews | Angela R. Neave own every finding, sign-off, submit

Two postures, one pipeline:
**A — Pre-trial exchange.** Many trial orders (incl. the firm's Lake-style
disclosure order) require the parties to EXCHANGE proposed final judgments /
settlement documents before trial — deadline from the intake ladder. This version
is our best-case ruling: it frames negotiations and shows the judge our theory.
**B — Post-trial submission.** After the hearing, per the judge's instruction
(format, deadline, Word to the JA, competing submissions) — capture the
instruction on the record at trial close and confirm via lal-judicial-procedures
(ask before web search).

## STEP 1 — SCOPE FROM THE PLEADINGS (hard rule)
List every claim actually pled and tried. The FJ addresses each — and nothing
more. Unpled relief in a proposed FJ is the classic reversible error; flag any
requested provision without a pleading anchor.

## STEP 2 — STRUCTURE (firm FJ master pattern — dissolution example; adapt for paternity/modification)
Caption; title FINAL JUDGMENT OF [DISSOLUTION OF MARRIAGE / PATERNITY / ...];
"THIS CAUSE came before the Court for trial on [dates]..." appearances recital.
Then issue-by-issue, each with FINDINGS then ORDERED paragraphs:
1. Jurisdiction/residency finding; 2. Marriage irretrievably broken (DOM);
3. **Parenting** — best-interest findings tied to § 61.13(3) factors as tried;
Parenting Plan incorporated (attach — lal-parenting-plan-builder version);
4. **Child support** — § 61.30 guidelines findings: incomes as found, worksheet
attached, deviations explained, retroactivity, arrears, income withholding;
5. **Equitable distribution** — § 61.075 identification/valuation/distribution
findings; the ED schedule attached; equalizer terms;
6. **Alimony** — § 61.08 need/ability + factor findings, form and duration
(post-July-2023 framework: no permanent alimony);
7. **Fees** — § 61.16 need/ability findings, amount, payment terms;
8. Reservation of jurisdiction; general provisions.
Numbers come ONLY from the trial worksheets/charts (lal-cs-worksheet,
lal-ed-chart, fee affidavit) — Russ cross-verifies every figure programmatically
against the source. A proposed FJ with an arithmetic error loses credibility on
every other paragraph.

## STEP 3 — EVIDENCE-ANCHORED FINDINGS (Posture B)
Each contested finding cites its support from the Issue Organizer / trial notes:
[Witness] testimony; Exhibit [#]. In the working draft, keep the anchors as
highlighted attorney notes; the submitted version's findings read clean.
Stipulated facts from the pretrial stipulation convert directly to findings.
Alternative provisions (Option A/B) highlighted wherever the attorney may want a
fallback ruling — per lal-pleading-standard, never guess; present alternatives.

## STEP 4 — DELIVERY + POST-JUDGMENT INTAKE
- Editable Word, formatting per lal-pleading-standard and the division's
  proposed-order requirements; exchange/submit by the deadline (attorney sends).
- **The moment ANY final judgment is RENDERED** (ours, theirs, or the court's
  own): calendar immediately — motion for rehearing (Rule 12.530) and appeal
  clocks run from rendition; route review to the attorney same day; route
  post-judgment lanes (rehearing / clarification / enforcement) to their skills.
  This intake is mandatory even when we win.
- Command center: exchange/submission logged; post-judgment clocks entered.
