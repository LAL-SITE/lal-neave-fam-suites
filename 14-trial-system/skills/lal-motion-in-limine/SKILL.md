---

## CONTROL BLOCK (from the firm master — hard rules)

- Do NOT use a motion in limine as a disguised summary judgment motion.
- Do NOT seek blanket exclusion of broad categories without evidentiary
  specificity — the challenged evidence must be SPECIFICALLY identified.
- Do NOT assume a pretrial ruling ends the matter: a limine ruling may still
  require a live objection at trial to preserve the issue; plan both.
- Do not rely on authentication alone — pair with hearsay and relevance grounds.
- Every requested exclusion must be NARROWLY TAILORED; distinguish complete
  exclusion from limited admissibility (admissible for X purpose, not Y).
- Check the trial order FIRST: many orders set a limine filing/hearing cutoff, and
  unheard motions may be waived. Calendar the hearing, not just the filing.

## STEP 1 — TARGET SELECTION (attorney decision, staff-prepared)

Pull Side B "Limine" items from the Objection Review Chart. The firm master's
high-value family-law categories:

undisclosed witnesses · undisclosed/late exhibits · hearsay and double hearsay ·
therapist records/letters · GAL materials · settlement and mediation
communications (90.408; mediation confidentiality) · privileged communications
(90.502–.5055) · texts/screenshots/social media lacking authentication or
completeness · financial summaries without § 90.956 compliance · improper
lay/expert opinion (90.701/90.702) · parenting coordinator communications · child
hearsay without 90.803(23) findings · criminal/dependency allegations · prior bad
acts and character attacks (90.404) · audio/video recordings (flag: interception
legality is an attorney/legal-research issue) · AI-generated or altered exhibits.

For each candidate, present the attorney: target evidence (specifically
identified), grounds (rule cites), strength, and the strategic cost (a limine
motion educates the other side — sometimes a live objection is better).

## STEP 2 — GROUNDS MODULES (build each argument per the master)

**Disclosure violation module:** deadline violated (order cite) → prejudice/surprise
→ bad faith or cure opportunity → remedy requested (exclusion / limited use /
continuance / conditions). Quote the order's own sanction language — the strongest
limine motion enforces the court's own order. Not every violation supports total
exclusion; request the proportionate remedy.
**Hearsay module:** identify the statement, declarant, purpose; analyze each layer
independently; anticipate their exception and rebut it element by element.
**Authentication module:** creator, preservation, alteration, metadata, chain of
custody, completeness (partial threads); heightened scrutiny for AI-generated or
edited material.
**Privilege module:** the privilege, its holder, no waiver; attorney-only analysis
on at-issue waiver — route the caselaw question to lal-caselaw-needs.
**90.403 module:** unfair prejudice / confusion / cumulativeness substantially
outweighing probative value; propose the limited-admissibility alternative.

## STEP 3 — DRAFT (firm master structure)

Court formatting per lal-pleading-standard; Neave Family Law Group, P.A. caption. Structure:

1. Introduction — movant, hearing/trial being protected, filing authority
2. Specifically identified evidence (a., b., c. — one item or tightly defined
   category per subparagraph; NO broad categories)
3. Grounds per item — rule-cited (hearsay / authentication / disclosure violation /
   privilege / relevance / unfair prejudice / cumulative), with the module analysis
4. Prejudice — why admission would materially impair fairness
5. Narrow tailoring — exactly what ruling is requested per item (exclude / limit
   purpose / require proffer outside the record / bar mention in openings)
6. WHEREFORE — exclusion, limitation, and/or evidentiary safeguards
7. Certificate of service; Neave Family Law Group, P.A. signature block

Clause bank (from the firm master — adapt): undisclosed witness ("not timely
disclosed in violation of the governing pretrial disclosure requirements");
screenshots ("lack adequate authentication and completeness safeguards");
settlement communications ("constitute inadmissible settlement discussions").
Caselaw: only verified project-file authority; otherwise
[CITE — Need #__, pending attorney verification] and a lal-caselaw-needs entry.

**Opposing THEIR motion in limine:** same modules in reverse — attack specificity
(blanket categories), show the evidence is disclosed/authenticated/excepted,
propose limited admissibility as the fallback, and preserve our proffer plan.

## STEP 4 — DEADLINES, HEARING, PRESERVATION

- Ladder entries: filing deadline, hearing date (set it — an unheard motion may be
  waived), ruling follow-up.
- Post-ruling: update the Objection Review Chart and Exhibit Tracker with what's
  excluded/limited; build the trial-notebook reminder — renew objections live where
  preservation requires; if OUR evidence is excluded, plan the proffer (12.470
  preservation).
- Command center: motion status + ruling logged in the Trial/Evidentiary Deadline
  Tracker.
