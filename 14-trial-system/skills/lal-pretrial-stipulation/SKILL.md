# Neave Family Law Group, P.A. Pretrial Stipulation / Pretrial Statement Drafter
## Staff Lane: Primary Paralegal drafts | Marta Estevez reviews | Angela R. Neave negotiate with OC, approve & sign
## Runs After: lal-trial-order-intake (Step 2A item 3 defines what this filing must contain)
## Trials only — evidentiary hearings rarely require this filing; confirm in the order before running

The pretrial stipulation is a strategy document dressed as a compliance filing.
The stipulated-facts section eliminates proof burdens; the disputed-issues section
frames the trial; the attached lists control what evidence exists. Drafting it is
paralegal work; deciding what to stipulate is ALWAYS attorney work.

---

## STEP 0 — GOVERNING ORDER CHECK

From the Trial Order Intake Memo (or the order itself if no intake has run):

- **Joint or separate?** "Joint Pretrial Stipulation" (conferral with OC required —
  build in conferral lead time) vs. each party's own "Pretrial Statement."
- **Due date** — filing deadline AND any earlier conferral deadline. If inside T-7,
  mark RUSH.
- **Required contents** — the order's list controls. Common required elements:
  statement/list of evidence, witness list, stipulated facts, disputed issues,
  pending motions, estimate of trial time, fee affidavits reference. Map each
  order-required element to a section; mark anything the order requires that the
  firm master doesn't cover.
- **Court's Uniform Trial Order language** — some orders (e.g., Broward UTO
  practice) prescribe the stipulation structure; follow the order over the master.

## STEP 1 — GATHER FROM THE MATTER FILE

- Procedural history: petition/counterpetition dates, service date, answer date
  (from filed pleadings — cite actual filing dates, never approximations)
- Prior partial agreements / mediation results (partial MSAs, agreed parenting
  provisions, Talking Parents or communication-platform agreements)
- Both parties' filed witness and exhibit lists (attach as Exhibits A and B)
- Pending motions (docket sweep — anything noticed but unheard)
- Each side's core positions for the disputed-issues section (our side from the
  attorney; their side from their pleadings and correspondence — stated neutrally)

## STEP 2 — DRAFT IN THE FIRM MASTER FORMAT (attorney-approved structure)

Court formatting per lal-pleading-standard. Neave Family Law Group, P.A. caption block (circuit ordinal,
county, CASE NO, DIVISION NO., Petitioner/and/Respondent, line-slash close).

Title: **JOINT PRETRIAL STIPULATION** (or [PARTY]'S PRETRIAL STATEMENT)

Opening (from the firm master): "The parties, Petitioner, [Name], and Respondent,
[Name], by and through their undersigned counsel, file this Joint Pretrial
Stipulation in accordance with the Court's [Uniform Trial Order / Order Setting
Trial dated ___] and state as follows:"

**Section structure (firm master — adapt to the order's required contents):**

1. **STATEMENT OF THE FACTS** — neutral case identification: action type, child(ren)
   with initials and DOB (initials only — confidentiality), who filed what and when,
   service and answer dates.
2. **STIPULATED FACTS** — only facts BOTH sides accept: residences, number of
   children, agreed parental responsibility, agreed logistics (travel notice,
   communication platform), anything resolved at mediation. Every candidate
   stipulation is an ATTORNEY DECISION — draft candidates highlighted, none final
   until approved. A stipulated fact requires no proof at trial; a wrongly
   stipulated fact is a concession that cannot be untried.
3. **STATEMENT OF DISPUTED ISSUES OF LAW & FACTS** — by party (MOTHER:/FATHER: or
   PETITIONER:/RESPONDENT:). Each side's contentions in short lettered paragraphs.
   Ours: framed by the attorney's theory of the case (tie each to the statutory
   factors — 61.13(3) timesharing, 61.30 support, 61.075 ED, 61.08 alimony,
   fees). Theirs: stated accurately from their pleadings — never strawmanned, never
   conceded.
4. **WITNESSES AND EXHIBITS** — "See attached [Petitioner's/Respondent's] Witness
   and Exhibit List attached hereto as Exhibit 'A'/'B'." Lists come from
   lal-witness-exhibit-lists; verify the versions attached are the operative
   (latest amended) ones.
5. **DEMONSTRATIVE AIDS** — disclose or state none, with the firm master's
   preservation line: "Neither party shall be precluded from utilizing a timely
   demonstrative aid at the trial."
6. **PENDING MOTIONS** — list every noticed-but-unheard motion or state none.
   Cross-check the docket; an omitted pending motion may be deemed abandoned.
7. **MEMORANDA AND LEGAL AUTHORITY** — per the master: "The [parties] will present
   respective legal authority at the time of trial," unless the order requires
   briefing now (then flag to attorney — that is a legal-research task for the
   browser Project).
8. **Reservation** — "The Parties reserve the right to amend and/or supplement this
   document." (Omit/adapt under strict-disclosure orders — check the regime
   classification from the intake memo.)

**Signature blocks (joint filing — side-by-side, per the firm master):** Neave Family Law Group, P.A. block
(attorney name, "Counsel for [Party]," firm address, phone, email) beside opposing
counsel's block. Joint filings are exchanged in Word with OC for signature —
attorney manages the exchange.

## STEP 3 — CONFERRAL WORKFLOW (joint stipulations)

1. Internal draft v1 → Marta Estevez → attorney approves our positions and stipulation
   candidates.
2. Attorney (or paralegal at attorney direction) sends Word draft to OC with the
   conferral deadline stated.
3. Track OC edits as a redline; every OC change to STIPULATED FACTS or OUR disputed
   issues goes back to the attorney — no silent acceptances.
4. Impasse on joint filing → per the order: file unilateral statements or note the
   disagreement; flag to attorney early, not at the deadline.

## STEP 4 — ATTORNEY FLAGS (every run)

- Every stipulated-fact candidate (with what proof burden it eliminates and what it
  concedes)
- Disputed-issue framing choices (Option A/B language where strategy differs)
- Pending motions that will be mooted or must be heard before trial
- Child confidentiality — initials/DOB format; no full child names (2.425)
- Mismatch between the order's required contents and the master structure
- Any stipulation touching support/ED numbers → cross-check against the current CS
  worksheet and ED chart before agreeing (route to lal-cs-worksheet /
  lal-ed-chart if stale)

## STEP 5 — QC, VERSIONING, DELIVERY

- Word document; caption/party designations verified against a filed pleading
- Attached Exhibits A/B are the operative filed lists (version-check)
- Draft tiers per lal-draft-versioning; highlights stripped only at
  lal-finalize-draft after attorney approval
- lal-prefiling-qc before signature; attorney signs and files — never staff
- Save to DRAFTS as `MM.DD.YY - LastName - Joint Pretrial Stipulation - v1.docx`;
  filed copy to the Trial/Hearing folder 00
- Command center: mark the pretrial statement deadline satisfied in the
  Trial/Evidentiary Deadline Tracker; log conferral status
- Time logged in {{PM_SYSTEM}} (drafting, conferral tracking, revisions)
