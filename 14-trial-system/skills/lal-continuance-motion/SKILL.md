# Neave Family Law Group, P.A. Motion for Continuance
## Paralegal drafts | Marta Estevez reviews | Angela R. Neave decide, obtain client signature, sign, argue
## Rule frame: Rule 12.460 → Fla. R. Gen. Prac. & Jud. Admin. 2.545(e)

A continuance motion is a time-critical filing that also signals case posture to
the court. The firm files them early, with specifics, and never as a substitute
for preparation.

## STEP 0 — RULE + ORDER COMPLIANCE CHECK (before drafting)
1. **Rule 2.545(e) elements** (every motion must contain): in WRITING; the reason
   for the continuance; whether the other party objects; when the case/hearing
   will be ready; and **signed by the party requesting it, except for good
   cause** — calendar getting the CLIENT'S signature (via the firm's e-signature process) or articulate the good cause for counsel-only signature.
2. **Rule 12.460 addition:** witness-unavailability grounds must state when the
   witness is believed to be available.
3. **The setting order's own continuance terms** — many trial orders layer
   requirements (joint status, specific format); the order controls; quote it.
4. **Conferral** — call/email OC first; state their position accurately (the firm
   exemplar states either the position or that conferral is not required, with
   why). An agreed motion should be presented as agreed with a joint proposed order.
5. **Timing** — file the moment the need is known. The firm exemplar was filed
   the business day the need crystallized, 3 days before the hearing, with the
   NOA filed contemporaneously. Same-day RUSH lane if inside T-7.

## STEP 1 — GROUNDS (firm exemplar pattern — concrete, not conclusory)
Numbered paragraphs, each a fact the judge can verify:
1. What is set, when, and on what pleading;
2. The specific circumstance (new counsel just retained with NOA filed
   contemporaneously; discovery outstanding through no fault; witness
   unavailable until [date]; medical; conflicting setting — attach proof);
3. What will happen during the continuance (the firm exemplar committed to
   filing the supplemental petition "within the next seven (7) days" — concrete
   commitments earn continuances);
4. Why proceeding now prejudices the client (due process, effective assistance,
   single comprehensive proceeding / judicial economy);
5. No prejudice to the other party — say why (or address their claimed prejudice);
6. Conferral/certification paragraph;
WHEREFORE — continue the [hearing/trial], readiness representation ("the matter
will be ready by ___" or "within __ days of [event]"), proposed order attached.
Certificate of service; firm signature block; CLIENT signature block when
required by 2.545(e).

## STEP 2 — OPPOSING THEIR CONTINUANCE
Compliance attack first (unwritten/unsigned/no readiness date/no reason), then
prejudice: our trial prep expenditures, witness availability locked, child-issue
urgency, pattern of delay (list prior continuances with dates). Attorney decides
oppose vs. agree-with-conditions (fees, discovery closure, no further
continuances term in the order).

## STEP 3 — AFTER RULING
Granted: lal-trial-order-intake RERUNS on the new setting order — the entire
Deadline Ladder rebuilds; old ladder entries closed, not deleted. Notify client,
witnesses, court reporter, and un-notice anything set (lal-correspondence).
Denied: trial prep continues on the existing ladder — flag any prep the motion
assumed would move.
