# Neave Family Law Group, P.A. Fee Affidavit & Fees-at-Hearing Prep
## Russ assembles billing data | Paralegal drafts | Marta Estevez reviews | The AFFIDAVIT IS THE ATTORNEY'S SWORN STATEMENT — Angela R. Neave review every line and sign

Fees in family court typically ride § 61.16 (need and ability to pay) or a
conduct/sanctions basis — the LEGAL basis is the attorney's call and shapes what
the affidavit must show. The staff job is a bulletproof factual record: hours,
rates, tasks, payments, and the exhibits behind them.

## STEP 0 — DEADLINE + BASIS
- Pull the fee-affidavit deadline from the Deadline Ladder (exchange deadlines of
  48 hours pre-trial are common — that is a HARD date with T-7/T-3 warnings
  already built by lal-trial-order-intake; if not, add them now).
- Confirm with the attorney: basis pled (§ 61.16 / contractual / sanctions-type),
  seeking and/or defending, and whether an expert fee witness will testify to
  reasonableness (some judges require one; check divisional practice via
  lal-judicial-procedures — ask before web search).

## STEP 1 — BUILD THE BILLING RECORD (Russ)
1. Export the full billing history from {{PM_SYSTEM}}: date, timekeeper, rate, task
   description, hours, amount; plus costs; plus payments/retainer applications.
2. Scrub line by line: privileged strategy detail in descriptions (redact
   narrative, keep task category — the affidavit and exhibits will be SERVED),
   block-billing entries flagged, duplicate/clerical entries flagged for the
   attorney's write-off decision. Never alter amounts — flag, don't fix.
3. Reconcile totals programmatically: billed − adjustments − payments =
   outstanding; plus estimated fees through trial (attorney provides).
4. Fee summary chart (lal-trial-charts-summaries patterns): fees by phase
   (pleadings / discovery / motions / mediation / trial prep), hours × rate by
   timekeeper, costs, payments, balance, projected-through-trial. One page.

## STEP 2 — DRAFT THE AFFIDAVIT (attorney's words, staff's scaffolding)
Court format per lal-pleading-standard. Contents scaffold:
affiant identity, bar number, role; retainer date and terms (attach retainer —
already standard trial exhibit #10 on firm exhibit lists); hourly rates and who
billed; summary of services by phase; total hours/fees/costs; payments received;
balance + projected trial fees; statement that rates and hours are reasonable
and necessary (the reasonableness factors analysis is the ATTORNEY'S — leave a
highlighted block for the attorney's own language; staff never draft the sworn
reasonableness opinion). Notarization/oath logistics per firm process.

## STEP 3 — THE FEES EVIDENCE PACKAGE
- Exhibits: retainer agreement + invoices (firm exhibit-list standard item:
  "Retainer Agreements and Invoices for purposes of attorney's fees") — redaction
  check (2.420/2.425 + privilege) before exchange.
- Witnesses: both Neave Family Law Group, P.A. attorneys are already standard entries on firm witness
  lists "– [Party]'s Attorney for fees"; confirm they are on the filed list; add
  any fee expert (expert-disclosure deadline check!).
- Need/ability proof: current FAs both sides, income analysis excerpts — route to
  Russ/lal-income-analysis; this is what wins § 61.16 fees, not just the invoices.
- Defending THEIR fee claim: obtain their affidavit and invoices at the exchange
  deadline; scrub for block billing, excessive hours, clerical time, and rates;
  build the challenge chart for cross.

## STEP 4 — EXCHANGE + CLOSE
Serve/exchange by the order's deadline (attorney approves; certificate per
practice). Update the Deadline Ladder, Exhibit Tracker (fee exhibits rows), and
command center. Save `MM.DD.YY - LastName - Fee Affidavit - v1.docx` to DRAFTS;
the billing export and summary chart to the Trial/Hearing folder 04. Time in
{{PM_SYSTEM}} — including this task (fees on fees are recoverable; unlogged work
isn't).
