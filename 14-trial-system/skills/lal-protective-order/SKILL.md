# Neave Family Law Group, P.A. Motion for Protective Order (Discovery / Subpoena)

## Feeds From: lal-witness-subpoenas (their improper subpoenas), lal-advanced-discovery (their improper requests), lal-evidence-objection-review (privileged targets)

Firm doctrine (from the master guide): **protective orders are precision tools, not
obstruction tools.** They prevent unfairness while proper discovery proceeds.
Overuse burns credibility with the court. The QC rule is absolute: **do not file if
the discovery is relevant, proportional, and legally permissible — even if it is
burdensome or unfavorable.** "Damaging" is not "protectable."

## STEP 0 — VIABILITY SCREEN (before any drafting)

Answer all six; if any fails, report to the attorney with the recommendation NOT
to file:

1. **Legally objectionable, not merely inconvenient?** Grounds: overbreadth,
   irrelevance to pled issues, privilege (90.502–.5055), confidentiality (trade
   secrets, third-party financials, minors' records), harassment/annoyance/
   embarrassment, undue burden or expense, prematurity, or disproportionality to
   the needs of the case (Rule 12.280 / 1.280(c)).
2. **Good-faith conferral done?** Required before filing. If not done, draft the
   conferral letter FIRST (lal-correspondence) and calendar the response window.
3. **Less intrusive alternative unavailable?** If a redaction, a confidentiality
   agreement, or a narrowed date range solves it, propose that in conferral.
4. **Deadline posture** — a motion aimed at a subpoena must move promptly, and in
   any event at or before the compliance time stated in the subpoena
   (Rule 12.410(c)); calendar from the subpoena's return date, not from convenience.
5. **Who moves** — party or affected non-party (we can move for a client OR a
   non-party we represent for this purpose; identify capacity in the caption
   paragraph).
6. **Standing discovery orders** — check for prior orders/CMO provisions that
   already govern the dispute.

## STEP 1 — SELECT THE POSTURE (one primary; firm master rule)

Select ONE primary protective-order posture; additional clauses only if supported
by the SAME factual and legal basis: prohibit the discovery entirely · limit scope
(topics/categories) · narrow the time period · protect privileged material
(privilege log posture) · confidentiality conditions (attorneys'-eyes-only,
use restrictions) · in camera review · sealing (coordinate Rule 2.420 procedure —
separate motion requirements) · cost-shifting · quash or modify the subpoena
(pair with 12.410(c) standards: unreasonable and oppressive).

## STEP 2 — DRAFT (firm master structure — follow exactly)

Court formatting per lal-pleading-standard; Neave Family Law Group, P.A. caption; title MOTION FOR
PROTECTIVE ORDER. Seven-part body from the firm master:

I. **Jurisdiction and procedural posture**
II. **The discovery or subpoena at issue** — date served, by whom, exactly what it
   seeks (quote the offending requests; attach as exhibit)
III. **Good cause for protection** — why the discovery is improper under Rule
   12.280 and/or Fla. R. Civ. P. 1.280(c): undue burden, expense, invasion of
   privacy, privileged/confidential disclosure, and/or disproportionality —
   FACTS, not adjectives (volume, cost, privilege basis, third-party impact)
IV. **Prejudice** — the concrete harm absent protection (expense, privacy,
   confidential/privileged disclosure, reputational harm, impaired participation)
V. **Good-faith conferral** — dates, method, outcome (attach the letter/email)
VI. **Narrow tailoring** — the relief is aimed at the specific discovery at issue
   and does not obstruct proper discovery
VII. **Relief requested** — the specific, tailored order sought
WHEREFORE — grant, protect, fees and costs where appropriate; certificate of
service; Neave Family Law Group, P.A. signature block.

Remove all unused clauses/placeholders. Caselaw: only verified project-file
authority; open questions (e.g., at-issue privilege waiver, non-party financial
privacy standards) → numbered lal-caselaw-needs entries with {{RESEARCH_PROVIDER}} prompts.

**Defending against THEIR protective order:** invert the screen — show relevance
to a pled issue and its statutory factors, proportionality, conferral attempts,
and offer tailored accommodations (narrowed scope, confidentiality agreement) that
moot their motion; flag fee-shifting exposure both directions.

## STEP 3 — HEARING + FOLLOW-THROUGH

- Set the motion for hearing (UMC or special set per the division's procedures —
  lal-judicial-procedures, ask before web search); a filed-but-unheard protective
  order motion protects nothing.
- Compliance is typically NOT automatically stayed by filing — flag to the
  attorney to seek a stay of compliance or agreement with OC pending hearing.
- After ruling: update the disco tracker, the subpoena status block, and the
  Trial/Evidentiary Deadline Tracker; if protection denied, calendar the
  compliance date immediately.
