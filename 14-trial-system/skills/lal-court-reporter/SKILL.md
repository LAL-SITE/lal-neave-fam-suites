# Neave Family Law Group, P.A. Court Reporter & Transcript Workflow
## Staff Lane: Paralegal books and tracks | Attorney approves cost and decides expedite | Marta Estevez reviews the plan on trial-bound matters

**Firm rule: no evidentiary proceeding happens without a reporter DECISION.**
Present ≠ default; absent ≠ default. The decision (book / rely on court recording /
waive) is made by the attorney per proceeding and logged. The record you didn't
make is the appeal you can't take.

---

## STEP 1 — THE REPORTER DECISION (at scheduling, not the week before)

Fires automatically from lal-trial-order-intake (Deadline Ladder task at
setting + T-14 confirmation) and from any hearing being calendared:

| Proceeding | Default | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Trial / final hearing | BOOK a reporter | FJ appeal/rehearing record |
| GM / Hearing Officer evidentiary hearing | BOOK — near-mandatory | Proceedings are typically only electronically recorded; Rule 12.490 review (exceptions/motion to vacate, 10-day clock) ordinarily requires a TRANSCRIPT of all relevant proceedings — no transcript, motion denied |
| Contested evidentiary hearing (temp relief, contempt, fees, relocation) | BOOK | Findings-heavy rulings; enforcement and appeal risk |
| Motion calendar / UMC, non-evidentiary | Attorney call | Cost vs. risk |
| Uncontested / agreed matters | Usually waive | Note the waiver |

Check the trial order and divisional procedures: some orders state reporter
arrangements are the parties' responsibility (the firm's Brevard exemplar does);
some GM divisions provide electronic recording only — confirm which via
lal-judicial-procedures (ask before web search).

## STEP 2 — BOOK AND CONFIRM

1. Attorney approves the cost (estimate: appearance fee + hourly; multi-day trials
   quote per day; client billing note per the retainer).
2. Book with the firm's vendor (from prior invoices / the How-To; capture:
   date(s), time, courtroom or Zoom link, judge/GM, estimated duration, case
   caption, our contact).
3. **Confirmation discipline:** written confirmation saved to the Trial/Hearing
   folder 05 (Court Logistics); T-7 reconfirm; T-1 reconfirm with the Zoom link or
   courtroom; reporter contact into the trial notebook logistics tab.
4. Remote hearings: confirm the reporter is on the Zoom invite and how they will
   swear witnesses / handle exhibits; flag audio quality responsibilities.
5. If opposing counsel booked the reporter: confirm scope and our right to order
   the transcript; never assume their reporter covers our record needs.

## STEP 3 — HEARING DAY

- Reporter checked in before start; appearance noted on the record
- Spellings sheet handed to the reporter (party names, witnesses, institutions,
  technical terms) — prepared from the witness list; saves transcript errors
- Exhibit handling agreed (marked exhibits referenced by number/letter on the record)
- Order the parts that matter while there: ask the reporter about rough-draft or
  expedited availability BEFORE leaving

## STEP 4 — TRANSCRIPT ORDERING (deadline-driven, never "when we get to it")

Compute the NEED-BY date first; the order type follows:

| Trigger | Transcript deadline driver |
|---|---|
| GM/HO recommended order received | Motion to vacate/exceptions clock (Rule 12.490 — 10 days from service). Order the transcript THE DAY the recommended order arrives; expedite is usually mandatory. If the proceeding was court-recorded only, request the recording/transcription through the division's procedure immediately |
| Rehearing / appeal consideration | 12.530 / appellate clocks from rendition — attorney call on ordering |
| Contempt enforcement of oral rulings | Before drafting the proposed order |
| Impeachment use at continued hearing | Before the next session |

Procedure: follow the firm How-To (TRANSCRIPT REQUEST) — request in writing with
case style, hearing date, judge/GM, courtroom, portions needed (full vs. excerpt),
format, and need-by date; get a cost + delivery estimate in writing; attorney
approves cost; calendar the promised delivery date with a T-3 follow-up; on
receipt, save to the matter (ORDERS or the hearing folder), quality-check names
and key rulings against notes, and route to the attorney.
