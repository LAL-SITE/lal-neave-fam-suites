# Neave Family Law Group, P.A. Witness Prep Packets
## Paralegal builds packets | Russ builds financial-testimony sections | Marta Estevez reviews | Angela R. Neave conduct every prep session and own every outline

**Prep ethics rule (non-negotiable):** we prepare witnesses to testify truthfully
and effectively — refresh recollection with documents, explain the process, and
rehearse HOW to testify (listen, answer only what's asked, don't argue, correct
mistakes immediately, "I don't know" is an answer). We never script answers or
suggest testimony. Any coaching concern → attorney immediately.

## PACKET TYPES

### A — OUR WITNESS: DIRECT PACKET (one per witness, call order from Tab 4)
1. **Witness header:** role, expected-testimony statement from the FILED list
   (the outline must stay inside it — strict-disclosure orders make this a
   boundary, not a suggestion), appearance mode (Zoom witnesses get the tech/oath
   logistics sheet), subpoena status.
2. **Direct outline — by ISSUE, not chronology:** for each Issue Organizer card
   the witness carries: the elements/statutory factors that issue needs
   (61.13(3)/61.30/61.075/61.08/61.16 anchors) → the facts THIS witness supplies
   for each → open-ended question stems (who/what/when/where/how — no leading on
   direct, 90.612 discipline) → the exhibits introduced through this witness with
   their **predicate scripts** (from the objection review's overcome plans: e.g.,
   photo predicate — "fairly and accurately depicts"; texts — participant
   authentication; business records — 90.803(6) elements or note the 90.902(11)
   cert already served).
3. **Vulnerability annex:** their anticipated cross themes (from Side B of their
   disputed-issues positions + the Issue Organizer weaknesses field), the bad
   facts this witness must own rather than dodge, prior inconsistent statements
   to reconcile BEFORE trial.

### B — THEIR WITNESS: CROSS PACKET
1. **Goals first (attorney sets, 3 max per witness):** what concessions this
   witness can give us; cross without a goal is noise.
2. **Control material:** every prior statement — deposition (page:line indexed),
   FA and discovery responses (party admissions, 90.801(2)), texts/emails,
   GAL/source statements — organized by topic for impeachment (90.608, 90.614:
   prior inconsistent statement procedure) and refreshing.
3. **Leading-question chains:** short, one-fact questions per goal, each chain
   ending before the "one question too many"; exhibit cites inline.
4. **Bias/credibility module:** relationship, financial interest, convictions if
   any (90.610 limits — attorney call), inconsistencies across their filings.

### C — CLIENT PREP SESSION PACKAGE (the client is Witness #1)
Session agenda for the attorney: process orientation (courtroom/Zoom mechanics,
who sits where, the judge's role — no jury); testimony rules card (plain-English
one-pager the client keeps); direct walk-through by issue; exhibit rehearsal —
the client handles every exhibit they will authenticate; mock cross on the three
hardest topics (attorney plays OC); logistics (arrival, dress, what to bring,
phone off, no reactions at counsel table). Schedule TWO sessions on the ladder:
substantive (T-10) and refresh (T-2). Client-facing instruction letter via
lal-correspondence (brand kit applies — plain English, Clarity/Options/Control).

## GUARDRAILS
- Child witnesses: NO prep, contact, or packet until the Rule 12.407 order exists
  — hard stop, attorney only.
- Expected-testimony boundary: if prep reveals the witness knows material things
  OUTSIDE the filed disclosure, flag immediately — amended list vs. exclusion
  risk is an attorney decision on a deadline.
- Non-client witnesses: prep sessions are attorney-conducted; staff coordinate
  scheduling and materials only.
- Packets live in the trial notebook Tab 4 and the Trial/Hearing folder — never
  in DISCO or any produced folder.
