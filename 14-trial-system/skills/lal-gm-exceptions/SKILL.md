From 1/1/2027: new trial after a jury verdict is **(a)**; rehearing is **(b)** and "may be directed only to a final judgment or final order"; court-initiated relief is **(c)**; the hearing provision is **(d)**; the motion to alter or amend moves to a later subdivision (letter not confirmed — **ATTORNEY VERIFY**).
> Unchanged in substance per the Committee Note: 15-day service windows; a challenge to missing required findings must still be raised by motion for rehearing (new **(b)(3)**, "Necessity of Motion"); nonjury sufficiency of the evidence needs no motion. New-trial orders must state grounds, **(a)(3)** — narrower wording than former (g), which also named rehearing orders.

## Companion: lal-court-reporter (the transcript IS the exceptions motion's oxygen)

The GM lane has three deadline traps, all short: the objection-to-referral window,
the 10-day exceptions/vacate clock from SERVICE of the recommended order, and the
transcript lead time that must fit INSIDE that clock. This skill exists so none of
the three is ever discovered late.

## POSTURE SELECTION (firm GM master system — classify first, never blend)
1. **Referral entered — accept or object?** Objection window is short (10 days is
   the standard framework — VERIFY against the referral's own language and current
   12.490/12.491 text; support HO matters under 12.491 differ from GM matters).
   Objection grounds (firm master): emergency posture, authority/complexity
   issues, credibility-heavy disputes, relocation, injunction overlap. Consent by
   silence — calendar the window THE DAY the referral arrives, decision to the
   attorney within 48 hours.
2. **GM/HO hearing prep** — a GM hearing IS an evidentiary hearing: the full
   trial-prep suite applies (intake ladder, lists, objection review, tracker) PLUS
   the reporter decision (near-mandatory — proceedings often electronically
   recorded only; review fails without a transcript).
3. **Recommended order received (ours to attack or defend)** — the core lane, below.
4. **Order adopting entered** — post-adoption attacks use vacate/rehearing lanes,
   not exceptions; attorney routing.

## THE RECOMMENDED-ORDER FIRE DRILL (same-day, non-negotiable)
The day a recommended order/report is served:
1. Jeri forwards (subject: "GM RECOMMENDED ORDER – [Client] – EXCEPTIONS CLOCK
   RUNNING"); paralegal calendars the **exceptions/motion-to-vacate deadline
   (10 days from service per 12.490(e) framework — verify against the order and
   current rule)** with T-7/T-3 warnings, plus the response window if THEY except.
2. **lal-court-reporter fires simultaneously**: order the transcript (expedited)
   or request the electronic recording per the division's procedure. The Garrido
   exemplar order says it plainly: review requires a record; "a record ordinarily
   includes a written transcript of all relevant proceedings"; without it the
   motion "will be denied."
3. Paralegal produces the 24-hour comparison memo: recommended order vs. our
   proposed disposition vs. the evidence — every adverse finding listed with the
   exhibit/testimony that contradicts it (from the Issue Organizer and trial
   notes). Attorney decides except / accept / negotiate within the window.

## DRAFTING EXCEPTIONS (firm GM master discipline)
Court format per lal-pleading-standard. **No generic disagreement** — the firm
master's rule: every exception identifies the EXACT finding, recommendation,
omission, legal error, evidentiary ruling, or due process defect challenged, with
a record cite (transcript page once available; exhibit number) and the specific
relief (vacate the finding, remand, substitute finding). Structure: caption;
EXCEPTIONS TO REPORT AND RECOMMENDED ORDER OF GENERAL MAGISTRATE (or motion to
vacate per the local vehicle — check the recommended order's own review
instructions and division practice); numbered exceptions, one defect each;
transcript status paragraph (ordered/attached); request for hearing per
procedure; WHEREFORE; certificate; signature block. Caselaw only from verified
project files; gaps → lal-caselaw-needs.
**Defending against THEIR exceptions:** compliance attack first (late,
transcript-less, generic), then answer each exception on the record — route via
lal-incoming-motion-response deadlines.
