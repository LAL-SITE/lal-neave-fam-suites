## 2. Statutory framework

- **Jurisdiction:** circuit court sitting in chancery (not the family division).
- **Venue:** county where the petitioner resides.
- **Residency:** petitioner must have lived in Florida **6+ months** before filing.
  Non-residents cannot file here. **This cannot be waived.** If a client is close to
  the 6-month mark, calculate the exact eligibility date and file on/after it.
- **Court discretion:** a name change is not an absolute right. Courts may deny for
  fraud/improper purpose, danger to the public or a person, a bad-faith pattern of
  changes, etc.

**§ 68.07 petition content** (every adult petition; parallel content for minor/family):
current legal name; proposed name; date and place of birth; father's name; mother's
maiden name; complete residence history from birth to present; spouse (if married)
and children (names/ages/residence); any prior name changes (when, where, which
court); occupation; current employer; 5-year employment history; business ownership;
profession/license; education; criminal-history statement; the § 68.07(3)(i)
sexual-predator/offender disclosure; bankruptcy and creditor-judgment statements;
the fingerprints/records-check paragraph; and the no-ulterior-purpose and
civil-rights statements. Signed and notarized by the petitioner.

**Minor service:** when one parent petitions, the other parent gets notice — by
**Consent** (Form 12.982(d)) if they agree, or by **service** if they don't (same (c)
petition either way). Non-resident other parent may require constructive notice by
publication (Ch. 49). A **UCCJEA Affidavit** is required whenever a minor's name is
at issue.

## 3. Case types

- **Adult individual** — 18+, changing own name. Fingerprints + background check.
- **Minor child** — parent/guardian petitions. Child fingerprinted (no check on
  child); petitioning parent/guardian fingerprinted + full check. UCCJEA required.
- **Family** — 2+ petitioners in one case; separate biographical section per
  petitioner; each petitioning adult gets a background check; one order covers all.
- **Name restoration** — restoring a *former* legal name (e.g., maiden name after
  divorce). **No fingerprinting/background check.** Otherwise standard.

---

## 4. Fingerprinting & background checks

- Adults and petitioning parents/guardians: fingerprints taken electronically at an
  FDLE-approved vendor → FDLE (state) → FBI (national). Results route to the clerk
  of court; **no hearing can be requested until the clerk has the results.**
- Minor child: fingerprinted, but **no background check is run on the child.**
- **Only exception:** name restoration (no fingerprinting at all).
- Cost $30–75, paid to the vendor. Can be done immediately after (or even before)
  filing. Results typically 1–3 weeks.
- ORI numbers and approved vendors are county-specific and change — see
  `ori-and-circuits.md` and **confirm live with the clerk** before sending client
  instructions.

## 5. Disqualifiers & red flags

**STOP — attorney escalation, do not proceed:**
- Sexual-offense conviction (any state, any time) or current predator/offender
  registry status.
- Non-resident / under 6 months Florida residency.
- Admitted fraudulent or improper purpose (evading debts, creditors, obligations).

**FLAG — proceed only with attorney direction:**
- Felony within 5 years; pattern of multiple prior name changes; minor case with a
  non-consenting parent (service required); active DV or custody-dispute context;
  name change sought right after a debt/judgment; competency concerns.

Document every flag in {{PM_SYSTEM}}. Absence of the other parent's consent does **not**
by itself block a minor petition — the standard is the child's best interest — but it
converts the matter to a service posture, which is a scope/escalation trigger.

## 6. Five-phase lifecycle + staffing (corrected 2026-07-17)

Name-change matters are **paralegal-run**. Attorneys review/sign, take a pre-hearing
call with the client, and attend the hearing (usually Zoom); they are pulled in
earlier only on escalations. Read older "Owner: Attorney" lines as
*paralegal-executed, attorney-reviewed.*

- **Phase 1 — Inquiry / Screening / Intake (Judith).** Identify case type, screen
  disqualifiers, verify residency, quote, retainer, payment, create {{PM_SYSTEM}} matter
  ("General - Name Change Adult" / "…Child"), send the correct intake form +
  fingerprinting instructions.
- **Phase 2 — Setup & Info-Gathering (Marta Estevez).** Review intake for completeness,
  verify 6-month residency, second-pass disqualifier screen, minor consent/service
  strategy, confirm filing county, identify the correct 12.982 form, brief attorney.
- **Phase 3 — Drafting & Filing (Marta Estevez drafts; attorney reviews/signs).** Populate
  the petition from intake, prepare the proposed Final Judgment, QC vs. the state
  form and the intake, attorney approval, e-file with the cover sheet (12.928),
  capture case number.
- **Phase 4 — Background Check & Hearing (Marta Estevez tracks; attorney: pre-hearing call +
  hearing).** Confirm the clerk has the background results, confirm the assigned
  judge's current procedures (live), draft/file the Notice of Hearing (#10), send
  the client hearing-instructions letter (#19).
- **Phase 5 — Final Order & Closing (Marta Estevez; attorney sign-off).** Judge signs the
  Final Judgment, order certified copies, send post-judgment next-steps letter (#22),
  close the matter, archive.

## 7. {{PM_SYSTEM}} 5-task workflow (matter type auto-triggers these)

1. **Gather Exhibits** — birth cert, ID, consents, any prior divorce Final Judgment.
2. **GET STARTED** — welcome email, intake form, draft pleadings, research county
   ORI, run the flat-fee invoice.
3. **E-filing Initial Pleadings** — Cover Sheet (12.928), Petition + Notice of
   Appearance + Email Designation + Notice of Related Cases; check judge/case number;
   set the background-check follow-up.
4. **Scheduling Hearing** — confirm the clerk has the background report, check the
   judge's procedures, draft/file the Notice of Hearing, send client hearing
   instructions.
5. **Getting Ready for Hearing** — build the hearing folder, upload to CMS, remind
   client & attorney, draft the Final Judgment but **do NOT upload it until after the
   hearing.**

Build note: minor/family and restoration variants of this workflow are on the backlog.

## 8. Timeline

6–10 weeks filing-to-order, typical: intake form ~5 business days; fingerprinting
1–3 weeks; drafting/filing 1–2 weeks; background clearance 1–3 weeks; hearing set
2–4 weeks after clearance; closing 1–2 weeks.
