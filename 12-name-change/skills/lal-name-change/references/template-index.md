# Master Template Index (#01–#23) + Florida Form Map

The 23 firm templates live in `/AI REFERENCE FILES/NAME CHANGE FORMS/MASTER
TEMPLATES - NAME CHANGE/` (README is `#00`). They are **firm-drafted drafting aids**
built to cover everything the matching Florida Supreme Court 12.982 form requires —
**not** the state forms themselves and **never** final pleadings. Every template uses
bracketed `[PLACEHOLDERS]` that an attorney fills and reviews before signing/filing.

**Always pull the live .docx from {{DMS}} before drafting** (via
`lal-file-connector`). If a caption ever shows an ALL-CAPS bold party name, it's an
outdated copy — re-pull.

## Which petition/judgment to use

| Situation | Petition | Final Judgment | Also required |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adult only | #01 (12.982(a)) | #13 (12.982(b)) | — |
| Minor's name changing, parent's not | #02 (12.982(c)) | #14 (12.982(e)) | UCCJEA #09; Consent #04 **or** service |
| Parent **and** minor both changing | #03 (12.982(f)) | #15 (12.982(g)) | UCCJEA #09; consent/service per child |
| Name restoration (former name) | #01, restoration language | #13 | **No fingerprinting** |

**Cover Sheet 12.928** accompanies the first pleading in every new matter — usually
auto-generated by the e-filing portal from the case-type selection; confirm per
county rather than drafting from scratch.

**Consent vs. service (minor):** 12.982(c) is the *one* minor petition, filed whether
or not the other parent consents. 12.982(d) (#04) is the Consent form the
non-petitioning parent signs. If consent can't be obtained, file the same (c)
petition and **serve** the other parent — no separate "without consent" petition
exists. Contested consent is a scope/escalation flag.

## Out of scope for this set
- **Report of Legal Name Change** (Vital Statistics form) — state-issued, completed
  post-judgment; not a firm template.
- **Fingerprint / background submission** — handled via the FDLE-approved vendor.

## Formatting truth (firm standard, from the de-identified master templates)
- Caption party name and the "IN RE:" line: **regular weight, normal case** — not
  bold, not ALL-CAPS.
- Wrapped numbered/lettered paragraphs **hang-indent** under the text (don't snap
  back to the margin).
- Adult petition body runs paragraphs 1–17 (present/proposed name; residence; birth +
  Exhibit A; parents incl. mother's maiden name; residence history since birth;
  family; former names; occupation + 5-yr employment; business; profession;
  education; criminal history + § 68.07(3)(i) statement; bankruptcy; creditor
  judgments; fingerprints/records-check paragraph; no-ulterior-purpose; civil
  rights), then the sworn-statement line, petitioner signature, notary block, and the
  firm signature block (Angela R. Neave FBN {{BAR_NO}}; Angela R. Neave, MS,
  Esq. Bar No. {{BAR_NO}}; 2400 E. Commercial Blvd., Suite 826, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33308; aneave@neavefamilylaw.com).

When a drafting skill or task produces one of these, reproduce this structure from
the live template — don't rebuild it from memory.
