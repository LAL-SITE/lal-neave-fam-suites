## Circuit-specific procedures (web-based scan 2026-07-17 — confirm with the JA)

- **Miami-Dade (11th) — no-hearing option.** Offers a Special Affidavit for Remote
  Name Change (Template **#20**) that lets a judge rule on an **uncontested** case
  **without a hearing.** This is a local practice — confirm current availability with
  the JA before telling a client a hearing can be skipped.
- **Sixth Circuit (Pasco/Pinellas) — standalone § 68.07(3)(i) affidavit.** Wants the
  sexual-predator/offender disclosure filed as a **standalone** affidavit (Template
  **#23**), filed as an amendment to the petition, not merely embedded in the petition
  body. This is a *disclosure* requirement, **not** a no-hearing procedure — don't
  confuse the two.
- **Checked and found no no-hearing procedure:** Seventeenth (Broward), Sixth
  (Pasco/Pinellas), Ninth (Orange/Osceola), Thirteenth (Hillsborough). As far as we
  can confirm, the no-hearing affidavit is **Miami-Dade only.** This was a
  web-search scan, not an exhaustive review of all 20 circuits' administrative orders
  — treat it as a lead, not a guarantee.

## Fingerprinting quick rules (see knowledge-base.md for detail)
- Adult petitioner and each petitioning parent/guardian: fingerprints **+** FDLE/FBI
  check.
- Minor child: fingerprinted, **no** check run on the child.
- Name restoration: **no** fingerprinting at all.
- No hearing can be requested until the **clerk** has the background results.
- Cost $30–75, paid by the client to the vendor.
