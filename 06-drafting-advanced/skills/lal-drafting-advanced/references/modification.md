# Modification Petition Drafting Reference

## What Can Be Modified
- **Timesharing / Parenting Plan** — requires showing substantial change in circumstances and that modification is in the best interests of the child
- **Child Support** — requires showing substantial change in circumstances (income change, child's needs, etc.)
- **Alimony** — requires showing substantial change in circumstances (modification) or specific triggering conditions (termination); depends on type of alimony in original order
- **Parental Responsibility** — requires substantial change + best interests showing

## Civil Cover Sheet Note
Modifications require a new Civil Cover Sheet and filing fee. Do not assume it
is optional. Flag for the person handling e-filing.

## Required Packet — Supplemental Petition for Modification
- Supplemental Petition for Modification of [Timesharing / Child Support / Parental Responsibility / Alimony]
- Civil Cover Sheet
- Financial Affidavit (if support or alimony is at issue)

## Required Allegations — Supplemental Petition for Modification
1. **Prior order** — Identify the order being modified (date, title, court, case number)
2. **Jurisdiction** — Court retains jurisdiction or re-establish as appropriate
3. **What was ordered** — Describe the specific terms of the prior order that are at issue
4. **Substantial change in circumstances** — Describe the specific, material, involuntary, and unanticipated changes (or the applicable standard for the type of modification)
5. **Best interests** — For timesharing modifications, allege that modification is in the best interests of the minor child(ren) (required)
6. **Relief requested** — Be specific; describe exactly what the modified arrangement / amount should be

## Substantial Change — What to Plead
Changes that typically support modification:
- Significant change in either party's income (support mods)
- Relocation of a parent or the child
- Change in the child's needs or circumstances
- Change in a parent's work schedule or availability
- Documented incidents affecting the child's safety or wellbeing
- Cohabitation or remarriage (for certain alimony modifications)
- Child reaching a milestone age affecting the arrangement

Do not generically say "circumstances have changed." Be specific. Use dates
and facts from the record and from the client.

## Service on Modification Petitions
A supplemental petition reopens a closed or ongoing case and **requires a new summons**,
just like an original petition. Do not assume the existing case means no new service
is required. Track service carefully.

## Timesharing Modification Specifically
Must allege:
- The current timesharing schedule (from the existing order — attach or quote it)
- The specific change in circumstances that has arisen since the order
- What new timesharing schedule is being requested (be specific — days, overnights,
  holidays, summers)
- Why the proposed schedule is in the best interests of the child

Best interests factors are governed by Florida statute. Do not fabricate the list —
note in Attorney Review Notes that best interests analysis should be confirmed.
