# Guardian ad Litem (GAL) Motion Drafting Reference

## What a GAL Is
A Guardian ad Litem (GAL) is a person appointed by the court to represent the
best interests of a minor child in a family law proceeding. The GAL is not the
child's attorney — the GAL represents the child's interests and reports to the court.

## When to Request a GAL
A GAL appointment may be appropriate when:
- There are serious allegations of abuse, neglect, or domestic violence
- Both parents are making contradictory allegations about the child's wellbeing
- A child has special needs that complicate the parenting dispute
- The parents' conflict is severe and the child is being placed in the middle
- A custodial evaluation alone is insufficient
- The court needs an independent investigation of the home environment

Flag all GAL requests for attorney review before filing.

## Motion for Appointment of Guardian ad Litem — Structure
```
MOTION FOR APPOINTMENT OF GUARDIAN AD LITEM

I. INTRODUCTION
Identify the parties and case. State that Petitioner/Respondent moves the
Court to appoint a Guardian ad Litem for the minor child(ren).

II. BACKGROUND
Numbered paragraphs — brief procedural history and nature of the dispute.
Identify the children (names and DOBs).

III. BASIS FOR APPOINTMENT
Numbered paragraphs addressing:
- The specific facts and circumstances that make GAL appointment appropriate
- Allegations that directly impact the child's best interests
- Why the Court's existing information is insufficient without a GAL investigation
- Any safety concerns or special needs of the child

IV. RELIEF REQUESTED
WHEREFORE, [party] respectfully requests this Court:
1. Appoint a Guardian ad Litem for the minor child(ren)
2. Direct the GAL to investigate [specific areas if known]
3. Award GAL fees as appropriate given the parties' financial circumstances
4. Grant such other relief as this Court deems just and proper
```

## Amended Motion for GAL
If a prior GAL motion was filed and the situation has changed, an amended
motion may be appropriate. Reference the original motion and explain what
has changed factually to support the amendment.

## GAL Fees
GAL fees are typically shared by the parties or allocated by the court. If
one party cannot afford GAL fees, note it in the motion and flag for attorney.
Pro bono GAL programs may be available through the circuit.

## Attorney Review Notes for GAL Motions
- Attorney must confirm factual basis meets the standard for appointment
- Attorney must confirm whether a custodial evaluation (under § 61.20) would
  be more appropriate or should be sought in addition to a GAL
- GAL appointment language should mirror the local circuit's preferred order form
