## SECTION I — PROPERTY, ASSETS, AND STRUCTURE

### Base Clause (Always Include)
All property owned prior to marriage shall remain the Separate Property of the owning party, including all appreciation, income, proceeds, and derivatives. Temporary commingling for convenience shall not alone create a gift or transmutation if the Separate Property may be reasonably traced. Separate Property may be traced through financial records, account statements, tax returns, bookkeeping data, or expert testimony.

### Business Interests Clause (Include If Either Party Owns a Business)
Business interests, including ownership, goodwill, enterprise value, retained earnings, and intellectual property, shall remain Separate Property absent a written agreement.

### Trust / Inheritance Clause (Include If Applicable)
No trust distribution, inheritance expectancy, or family wealth interest shall create a marital interest absent an express written instrument.

## SECTION II — INCOME AND FINANCIAL FLOW

### Base Clause (Always Include)
Each party's earnings, income, and compensation during the marriage shall remain Separate Property unless expressly agreed otherwise in writing. Passive income, investment returns, and reinvested earnings shall retain their original character unless intentionally converted. No implied gift or conversion shall arise from joint use or access absent clear written intent.

## SECTION III — SUPPORT (ALIMONY)

**Select exactly ONE — remove the others before finalization.**

### Option A — Full Waiver
The parties mutually and voluntarily waive all rights to alimony, spousal support, or maintenance of any kind, whether temporary, periodic, rehabilitative, durational, or permanent, including any future right to seek modification. This waiver is knowing, voluntary, and made after full financial disclosure or express written waiver of the right to further disclosure. The parties acknowledge that future circumstances may change and nevertheless agree to this waiver. This waiver shall remain enforceable notwithstanding any change in circumstances.

### Option B — Limitation
The parties agree that alimony, if any, shall not exceed [AMOUNT] per month for a period not to exceed [DURATION]. This limitation is knowing, voluntary, and made after adequate financial disclosure. The parties acknowledge that future circumstances may change and nevertheless agree to this limitation.

### Option C — Conditional
Alimony obligations under this Agreement shall be triggered only upon the occurrence of the following conditions: [CONDITIONS]. If the stated conditions do not occur, neither party shall have any obligation for alimony or spousal support. This structure is knowing, voluntary, and made after adequate financial disclosure.

### Option D — Reservation
The parties agree that the right to seek alimony or spousal support is expressly reserved. Neither party waives any right to seek alimony in the event of dissolution of marriage. The court retains full jurisdiction to consider any claim for alimony under applicable Florida law.

## SECTION IV — ESTATE AND DEATH RIGHTS

### Base Clause (Include When Estate Rights Are Addressed)
Each party waives all elective share, intestate rights, exempt property rights, family allowance, homestead rights, and fiduciary appointment rights arising from the marriage, except as otherwise provided. The parties acknowledge that estate planning documents should be consistent with this Agreement and may be updated separately.

## SECTION V — DEBTS AND INDEMNIFICATION

### Base Clause (Always Include)
Each party shall remain solely responsible for his or her individual debts, whether incurred before or during the marriage, unless otherwise agreed. Each party shall indemnify and hold the other harmless from any liability, claim, or obligation arising from such debts. Unknown or undisclosed liabilities shall remain the responsibility of the incurring party.

## SECTION VI — ENFORCEMENT AND LEGAL FRAMEWORK

### Fees — Prevailing Party (Neave Family Law Group, P.A. Default)
The prevailing party in any action concerning enforcement or interpretation of this Agreement may recover reasonable attorney's fees and costs.

### Fees — Own Fees (Use Only If Attorney Directs)
Each party shall be responsible for their own attorney's fees and costs in any action concerning this Agreement.

### Standard Provisions (Always Include)
Each party acknowledges that no reliance has been placed on representations not contained in this Agreement. If any provision is determined unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect. The parties acknowledge that this Agreement is intended to remain enforceable despite future changes in circumstances. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Florida.

## SECTION VII — HIGH-RISK AND ENFORCEABILITY PROTECTIONS

### Include When Any Risk Factor Is Present
The parties acknowledge that this Agreement has been entered into freely and voluntarily, after full opportunity for review and consultation with independent counsel. The parties further acknowledge that disparities in financial circumstances, timing of execution, and future uncertainty have been considered. The Agreement is intended to be fair, reasonable, and enforceable under Florida law. Any ambiguity shall be interpreted in favor of enforceability.
