## Universal Mandatory Clauses — All Evaluation Orders

Every evaluation order must include:

- [ ] **Identification of the evaluator** (or process for selecting one)
  > "The parties shall select a mutually agreed evaluator within [X] days.
  > Failing agreement, the GAL/Court shall select."

- [ ] **Scope of the evaluation**
  > "The evaluator is authorized to evaluate [named party/child] for the
  > purpose of [specific purpose as ordered]."

- [ ] **Release of records authorization**
  > "The evaluator is authorized to obtain records from [categories] including
  > but not limited to: school records, medical records, mental health records,
  > DCF records, and law enforcement records."

- [ ] **Cooperation requirement**
  > "Both parties shall fully cooperate with the evaluator and provide
  > releases as requested."

- [ ] **Collateral contacts authorization**
  > "The evaluator is authorized to contact collateral sources, including
  > [specify categories] as deemed appropriate."

- [ ] **Report delivery**
  > "The evaluator's written report shall be provided to [GAL / Court / Counsel]
  > within [X] days of completion."

- [ ] **Cost allocation**
  > "The cost of the evaluation shall be [shared equally / paid by [Party] /
  > addressed by subsequent order]."

- [ ] **Report confidentiality**
  > "The evaluation report is confidential and shall not be disclosed except
  > as directed by the court."

## Psychological Evaluation — Additional Clauses

- [ ] Specify adult evaluation vs. child evaluation vs. both
- [ ] Authorization for psychological testing (specify battery if required)
- [ ] Authorization for review of prior evaluations
- [ ] Prohibition on ex parte contact with evaluator by parties

---

## Substance Abuse Evaluation — Additional Clauses

- [ ] Specify evaluator credential requirements (LCSW, CAP, CCAP, etc.)
- [ ] Authorization to obtain prescription monitoring records (PDMP)
- [ ] Testing protocol (hair follicle / urine / ETG — specify)
- [ ] Follow-up testing authorization (if initial positive result)
- [ ] Treatment compliance reporting requirement

---

## Psycho-Educational Evaluation — Additional Clauses

- [ ] Specify child and grade level
- [ ] Authorization to contact school directly
- [ ] Specify testing to be administered (academic, cognitive, processing)
- [ ] Report to school (whether and how)
- [ ] IEP review authorization (if applicable)

## Key Clause — GAL Access to Evaluation Report

> ⚠️ This clause is critical. Without it, the GAL may not receive the report.

```
The Guardian ad Litem shall receive a copy of the evaluator's complete
written report upon completion. The evaluator is authorized to communicate
directly with the Guardian ad Litem regarding findings relevant to the
best interests of the minor child(ren).
```

## Common Drafting Errors — Evaluation Orders

| Error | Correct Approach |
|-------|----------------|
| Evaluator named without agreement process | Include fallback selection process |
| Scope too vague ("psychological evaluation") | Specify adult / child / forensic / testing battery |
| No cost allocation | Always address cost — even if deferred |
| No GAL copy provision | Include GAL access clause |
| No timeline | Include deadline for evaluation completion |
| No cooperation clause | Always include cooperation requirement |
