produces starting drafts, not final documents.

**Minimum required for every document:**

- Respondent name and role

## Step 2 — Document Templates

---

### 1A — GAL CONTRACT

**Additional inputs needed:**
- Date of contract
- Hourly rate
- Fee split (per court order)
- Full legal names of both parties

**Structure:**
1. Header: "GUARDIAN AD LITEM CONTRACT"
2. Opening: parties identified, case number, appointment order date
3. ¶1 — Appointment and Acceptance: acknowledge appointment; name minor children with DOBs; reference court order
4. ¶2 — Scope of Services: investigate; interview; review records; prepare and file reports; attend hearings
5. ¶3 — Fees and Payment:
   - (a) Hourly rate
   - (b) Fee split per court order
   - (c) Retainer / advance payment terms
   - (d) Billing cycle and invoice process
   - (e) Consequences of non-payment
6. ¶4 — Confidentiality
7. ¶5 — Term and Termination
8. Signature blocks: GAL, Petitioner (with date), Respondent (with date)

### 2A — MOTION TO COMPEL PAYMENT OF GAL FEES

**Additional inputs needed:**
- Appointment date and fee order
- Hourly rate and fee split
- Total billed to date
- Amount each party owes / has paid / is delinquent

**Structure:**
1. Court caption
2. Title: "MOTION FOR ORDER COMPELLING PAYMENT OF GAL FEES AND FOR ATTORNEY'S FEES AND COSTS"
3. COMES NOW paragraph

   - ¶5: Payments made; each party's balance
   - ¶6: Invoices issued; no objections received; reference Composite Exhibit B
   - ¶7: Request for advance retainer for remaining work (if applicable)
   - ¶8: Request for attorney's fees for bringing this motion
5. WHEREFORE clause
6. Certificate of Service
7. Signature block (see Step 3 below for correct format)

### 2C / 3A — MOTION AND ORDER: FORENSIC PSYCHOLOGICAL EVALUATION — PARENT

**Additional inputs needed:**
- Which parent(s) to be evaluated
- Name and contact of proposed evaluator (if known)
- Factual basis for the evaluation (from case investigation — do not fabricate)
- Whether agreed or contested

**⚠️ CRITICAL LANGUAGE — FORENSIC SPECIFICATION:**
The motion and order MUST use the term **"forensic psychological evaluation"** —
not "psychological evaluation," not "mental health evaluation," not "clinical evaluation."
The order MUST require the evaluator to review collateral sources including:
the GAL report, all prior court filings, any video or audio evidence, prior mental
health records, and all other materials the GAL has compiled.

See `references/evaluation-order-key-clauses.md` for mandatory clause checklist.

**Order numbered paragraph structure (per project template):**
- ¶1: Evaluator identified (name, credentials, address, phone, email)
- ¶2: Evaluator shall conduct a **forensic** psychological evaluation; shall perform whatever interviewing and testing evaluator deems necessary
- ¶3: Purpose: assist the Court with respect to parental fitness and best interest of the children
- ¶4: Collateral sources: evaluator SHALL review — GAL report, prior court filings, video/audio evidence, prior mental health records, and all materials compiled by the GAL
- ¶5: Parent, past/present mental health providers, and any other individuals evaluator deems necessary shall be interviewed
- ¶6: Parent shall execute all releases requested by evaluator
- ¶7: Parent shall contact evaluator within [5] days; shall cooperate fully; shall attend all appointments
- ¶8: Evaluator shall provide written report simultaneously to counsel for each party and the GAL
- ¶9: Raw testing data kept confidential; maintained by evaluator per professional standards; released only to qualified expert per Fla. Admin. Code 64B19-19005 and Fla. Stat. Chapter 490
- ¶10: Cost allocation

---

### 2D / 3B — MOTION AND ORDER: PSYCHOLOGICAL/EDUCATIONAL EVALUATION — MINOR CHILDREN

**Structure mirrors 2C/3A with key differences:**
- ¶1: Names evaluator for the minor children
- ¶3: Purpose is to gather information about the educational and psychological state of the minor children and make treatment plan recommendations
- ¶5: Minor children's past/present mental health providers shall be interviewed
- ¶8: Report provided simultaneously to counsel and GAL; raw testing data kept confidential per Fla. Admin. Code and Fla. Stat. Chapter 490

---

### 2E / 3C — MOTION AND ORDER: SUBSTANCE ABUSE EVALUATION

**Additional inputs needed:**
- Which parent to be evaluated
- Name and contact of proposed provider (if known)
- Basis for evaluation (specific documented concerns — not bare allegations)

**Order numbered paragraph structure (per project template):**
- ¶1: Provider identified; parent to be evaluated
- ¶2: Provider shall conduct substance abuse evaluation; shall perform whatever interviewing and testing deemed appropriate; shall request laboratory testing (urine, hair follicle, nail testing); parent shall comply within 24 hours; parent shall direct testing facility to remit results directly to provider
- ¶3: Purpose: assist Court with whether parent has substance/alcohol addiction; recommendations as to therapeutic/treatment plan
- ¶4: Provider shall provide Statement of Understanding to parent's counsel within [48] hours; parent shall execute within [24] hours
- ¶5: Parent, past/present mental health care providers, and other individuals deemed necessary shall be interviewed
- ¶6: Provider may obtain information from mental health professionals; parent shall execute all releases
- ¶7: Parent shall contact provider within [5] days; shall fully cooperate
- ¶8: Written report provided simultaneously to counsel and GAL
- DONE AND ORDERED

## AMBIENT STANDARD — Neave Family Law Group, P.A. PLEADING FORMAT & DRAFT-STATE RULES

`lal-pleading-standard` governs every court-bound document this skill produces.
Two rules, from the first keystroke:

1. **Format correctly immediately** — portrait, Times New Roman 12 black, single
   spacing 0pt before/after, left aligned, 1" margins, Neave Family Law Group, P.A. caption (court centered ·
   case/division right · parties left · rule line ending in `/` · title centered
   bold ALL CAPS), numbering I. → 1. → a. → i. continuous. No bullets, no dividers.
   There is no rough-format stage that gets fixed later.
2. **Drafts show their work, highlighted** — attorney notes 🟨, outstanding facts 🟨,
   alternative provisions 🟩 drafted in full. When unclear what the pleading should
   contain, present highlighted ALTERNATIVES — never assume and draft one version
   as if decided. Never strip flags at draft stage; only `lal-finalize-draft`
   strips, after the attorney resolves them.

## MANDATORY HANDOFF — PRE-FILING QC GATE

Any document this skill produces that will be **filed with a court, served on a
party, or sent outside the firm** goes to `lal-prefiling-qc` before it reaches
an attorney. This handoff is not optional and is not skippable for urgency.

```
THIS SKILL  →  lal-prefiling-qc  →  ATTORNEY SIGN-OFF  →  FILE / SERVE / SEND
```

- Do not present a draft as "ready for Angela R. Neave" or "ready to file" until the gate has run.
- A **BLOCKED** verdict returns the draft here for correction. Fix and re-run the gate.
- A **CONDITIONAL** verdict goes to the attorney with the findings attached.
- Never resolve a flagged item by deleting the language to make the gate pass.

- After the gate clears, hand the document to `lal-finalize-draft` to produce the
  filing-ready Word file and PDF. That skill enforces portrait, Times New Roman 12,
  all black, single spacing, the Neave Family Law Group, P.A. caption layout, strips every internal artifact,
  and checks `lal-judicial-procedures` for the assigned judge before output.

**Approval behavior:** if the person in the session is an Neave Family Law Group, P.A. attorney (Angela R. Neave or
Angela R. Neave), her instruction IS the sign-off — do not ask her to approve what she just
directed. If staff, every attorney-gated item routes to Angela R. Neave and waits.
