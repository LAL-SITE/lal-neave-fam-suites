## Information Classification Labels

Every piece of information in this case must be classified using one of the following labels.
Apply consistently across all outputs.

| Label | Definition | When to Use |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| **ALLEGATION** | Unverified claim made by a party | One party asserts something without corroboration |
| **REPORTED STATEMENT** | What a person said, attributed to them | Direct account from a source — parent, collateral, child |
| **DOCUMENTED** | Reflected in a record or document | Medical records, school records, court filings, texts |
| **CORROBORATED** | Supported by two or more independent sources | Multiple sources confirm the same fact independently |
| **DISPUTED** | Parties or sources give conflicting accounts | Accounts directly contradict each other |
| **UNKNOWN / MISSING** | Not yet known or records not yet received | Gap in investigation |
