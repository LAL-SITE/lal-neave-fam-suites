## When to use this skill (full trigger reference)

GAL Investigation Tracker for Florida Guardian ad Litem matters. Use this skill whenever the GAL needs to track, update, or report on the status of an active investigation. Triggers on any of the following: "update the investigation," "what's still outstanding," "what have I done so far," "add a contact," "mark this record received," "who haven't I talked to yet," "what's missing," "I just interviewed," "I got records from," "generate an investigation status memo," "what do I still need," or any request to log, review, or summarize investigation activity. Also triggers before any report drafting — always run this first to confirm investigation completeness before generating interim or final reports. This skill is the running log of the investigation — it must be updated after every significant investigative activity.

# GAL Investigation Tracker Skill

## Purpose

This skill maintains the **Investigation Status Log** — the living record of everything
the GAL has done, what remains outstanding, and what gaps exist. It feeds directly into
the "GAL's Investigation" section of every report and is the primary tool for ensuring
no source is missed and no gap is left unaddressed.

A GAL whose investigation is incomplete when called to testify is vulnerable. A GAL
whose investigation log is current, detailed, and gap-aware is protected.

---

## Two Modes of Operation

### MODE A — UPDATE
New information provided (interview completed, record received, contact attempted).
Update the log and output the revised status.

### MODE B — STATUS REVIEW
The GAL asks what is pending, missing, or outstanding.
Generate a current status report and gap analysis.

If both → do both.

---

## The Investigation Log — Five Sections

Always output all five sections when generating a status report, even if some are empty.

---

### SECTION 1 — PERSONS INTERVIEWED

Track every person contacted or attempted.

| Field | Options |
|-------|---------|
| Name / Role | e.g., "Mother," "Father," "Dr. Smith, child's therapist" |
| Contact Method | In person / Phone / Email / Text / Video / Combination |
| Date(s) of Contact | List all dates |
| Status | COMPLETE / PARTIAL / PENDING / ATTEMPTED — NO RESPONSE / PHONE TAG |
| Home Visit | YES / NO / N/A |
| Notes | Key issues raised, follow-up needed, resistance encountered |

**Status definitions:**
- **COMPLETE** — Interview conducted, notes captured, no outstanding follow-up
- **PARTIAL** — Contact made but additional interview needed
- **PENDING** — Not yet contacted; on the list
- **ATTEMPTED — NO RESPONSE** — Documented attempts made, no response received
- **PHONE TAG** — Mutual attempts, no successful connection yet

⚠️ Flag any person with 3+ documented attempts without response. This is a reportable
finding in the GAL's investigation section.

---

### SECTION 2 — RECORDS LOG

Track every record requested, received, reviewed, or outstanding.

| Field | Options |
|-------|---------|
| Record Type | e.g., "School records — [School Name]," "Mental health — Dr. Jones" |
| Provider / Source | Name and contact |
| Request Sent | Date (or PENDING) |
| Authorization Signed | YES / NO / N/A |
| Received | Date (or OUTSTANDING) |
| Reviewed | YES / NO |
| Notes | Key findings, gaps, follow-up needed |

**Record types to track:**
- School records (attendance, grades, behavioral incidents, IEP if applicable)
- Medical records (pediatrician, specialists)
- Mental health records (therapist, psychiatrist, treatment facilities)
- DCF records / prior investigations
- Law enforcement records (DV reports, criminal history)
- Substance abuse records (evaluation, treatment)
- Prescription monitoring (PDMP)
- Parenting coordinator records / reports
- Prior court orders and GAL reports
- Communication logs (Talking Parents, OurFamilyWizard, text logs)
- Other (specify)

⚠️ Flag any record requested but not received within 30 days.
⚠️ Flag any record critical to a key allegation that has NOT yet been requested.

---

### SECTION 3 — DOCUMENTS REVIEWED

Track all pleadings and documents reviewed (distinct from records requested from third parties).

For each document:
- Document name / type
- Date received or reviewed
- Source (party, court, counsel)
- Key issues raised

---

### SECTION 4 — ACTIVE GAPS

The most important section for investigation integrity.

**Gap types:**
- **MISSING SOURCE** — A person who should be contacted has not been
- **MISSING RECORD** — A relevant record has not been requested or received
- **UNRESOLVED INCONSISTENCY** — Two sources conflict; resolution pending
- **UNSUPPORTED ALLEGATION** — An allegation exists with no corroborating source yet identified
- **INCOMPLETE INTERVIEW** — Contact made but interview not completed
- **UNANSWERED QUESTION** — A specific question raised remains open

For each gap, state:
1. What is missing
2. Why it matters (which allegation or issue it bears on)
3. What action is needed
4. Priority: **HIGH** / **MEDIUM** / **LOW**

**Priority guidelines:**
- HIGH — Safety concern, or directly bears on the GAL's primary recommendations
- MEDIUM — Relevant to findings but not outcome-determinative alone
- LOW — Supporting detail; helpful but not critical

---

### SECTION 5 — INVESTIGATION STATUS SUMMARY

```
INVESTIGATION STATUS — [Case Number] — [Date]

Overall Status: [ ] EARLY STAGE  [ ] IN PROGRESS  [ ] SUBSTANTIALLY COMPLETE  [ ] COMPLETE

Parties Interviewed:       Mother [ ]  Father [ ]  Child(ren) [ ]
Home Visits Complete:      Mother [ ]  Father [ ]
Key Collaterals:           [#] of [#] identified contacted
Records Requested:         [#] of [#] identified requested
Records Received:          [#] of [#] requested received
Active Gaps:               [#] HIGH  [#] MEDIUM  [#] LOW
Outstanding Phone Tag:     [list names or "None"]
Next Priority Actions:     [top 3 items to address]
Report Readiness:          [ ] NOT READY  [ ] INTERIM ONLY  [ ] READY FOR FINAL
```

---

## Updating the Log (MODE A)

When new information is provided:
1. Identify the update type (new contact, record received, document reviewed, gap identified, gap resolved)
2. Update the correct section
3. Check for gap resolution — remove from Active Gaps and note as resolved with date
4. Check for new gaps — add to Active Gaps if new follow-up identified
5. Update the Status Summary
6. Output the updated log in full with changes indicated

---

## Generating a Status Report (MODE B)

Output in this order:
1. Investigation Status Summary (Section 5)
2. Persons Interviewed — highlight PENDING or ATTEMPTED — NO RESPONSE
3. Records Log — highlight OUTSTANDING or unrequested critical records
4. Active Gaps — sorted HIGH → MEDIUM → LOW
5. Next Priority Actions — the three most important things to do next

Then add a **Report Readiness Assessment:**

> Based on the current status, this matter is [NOT READY / READY FOR INTERIM ONLY /
> READY FOR FINAL REPORT] because: [state specific reasons]

**Do NOT declare ready for final report if:**
- Either parent has not been interviewed
- The child has not been interviewed (unless developmental age precludes it — note that)
- Either home visit is outstanding
- Any HIGH-priority gap remains open
- Any record directly bearing on a key allegation has not been received and reviewed

---

## Integration with Reports

When the GAL's investigation section of a report is being drafted, this log feeds:

- **"Reviewed the following documents"** → Section 3 (Documents Reviewed)
- **"Communicated with the following non-parties"** → Section 1 (COMPLETE or PARTIAL)
- **"Left messages or played phone tag with"** → Section 1 (ATTEMPTED / PHONE TAG)
- **"Records requested"** → Section 2

---

## Completeness Standard

A thorough investigation reaches out to everyone identified by both sides and documents
what each person said — including witnesses whose accounts were neutral or inconclusive.
A GAL who only interviewed witnesses whose accounts supported one side is vulnerable to
a cross-examination argument that the investigation was selective and biased.

- Contact attempts must be documented even when unsuccessful
- Neutral or unhelpful collateral sources must still appear in the log
- Contrary accounts must be recorded, not omitted
- Every identified source must have an entry — even if the entry is PENDING

---

## Output Standards

- Use exact dates for all entries
- Never mark a source COMPLETE unless the interview has been conducted and notes captured
- Never mark a record RECEIVED unless obtained and reviewed
- Do not omit sources because their accounts were unhelpful
- Do not close gaps prematurely — a gap is only closed when the specific missing information
  has been obtained from a credible source
- Reflect actual status, not aspirational status
