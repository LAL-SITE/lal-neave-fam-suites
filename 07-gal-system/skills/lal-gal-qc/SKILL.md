# GAL Quality Control Skill

## Purpose

This skill is the integrity gate for every document that leaves the GAL's desk.
It does not draft — it audits. It reads what was written and tells the GAL exactly
what is wrong, what is missing, what is inconsistent, and what will not survive
cross-examination.

**Scope:** Reports (interim and final), motions, proposed orders, contracts, letters,
emails, chronologies, source summaries, evidence review sections — any work product.

**Output:** A structured QC Report with findings organized by severity, specific flagged
text, and required corrections.

## The QC Modules

---

### M1 — FACTUAL ACCURACY AND INTERNAL CONSISTENCY

**1a. Case identification data**
- Case number: consistent throughout
- Court, circuit, county, division: correct and consistent
- GAL name and credentials: correct spelling, correct title (MS, Esq.)
- Appointment order date: consistent with case intake profile
- Report number (First, Second, Third, etc.): matches actual filing sequence

**1b. Children**
- ⚠️ CHILDREN'S NAMES RULE: Minor children are identified by **initials only** in the
  body of court filings (e.g., "A.B."). Full names appear ONLY in the appointment
  paragraph (first identification with DOB) and in the GAL Contract.
  Flag any use of a child's full name in the body of a court filing. → 🔴 CRITICAL
- DOBs: verify against case intake profile — no transposed digits
- Ages: verify ages are current as of the report date; must match DOB

**1c. Party names**
- Petitioner and respondent spelled consistently throughout
- Roles (Mother/Father/Petitioner/Respondent) consistent throughout
  Flag any place where a party's role switches mid-document
- Formal filings: refer to parties by role, not first name (unless template uses first names,
  e.g., initial letter)

**1d. Date consistency**
- All dates internally consistent
- Flag any date that precedes the appointment date
- Flag any future date stated as having already occurred
- Flag any date stated as approximate that should be verifiable

**1e. Cross-document consistency**
Where case intake profile, investigation log, or prior reports are available:
- Does appointment date match the intake profile?
- Do children's initials/DOBs match?
- Do listed sources match the investigation tracker?
- Do recommendations align with or explain departure from prior recommendations?

---

### M2 — NAMING AND IDENTIFICATION CONVENTIONS

**2a. Minor children — court filings**
✅ Correct: "A.B. (age 10)" or "the minor child A.B."
❌ Flag: Full name used in body of a court filing

**2b. Minor children — internal documents**
Full names acceptable in GAL Contract and internal working documents not filed with the court.
Flag if full names appear in a document marked CONFIDENTIAL being attached to a court filing.

**2c. Party references — formal documents**
✅ Correct: "Mother," "Father," "Petitioner," "Respondent"
❌ Flag: First names only in formal report sections (e.g., "Brian reported" → should be
"Father reported" or "Petitioner reported")
Exception: Initial letter to parties uses first names in salutation.

**2d. Third parties and collaterals**
Court filings: use role/relationship unless there is a specific reason to name the individual.
Family members: use relationship designation (e.g., "Maternal Grandfather (MGF)").

**2e. Evaluator and professional references**
First reference in report text: full name and role.
Subsequent references: last name or role.
In proposed orders: full name, credentials, and contact information required.

---

### M3 — SOURCE ATTRIBUTION AND ALLEGATION DISCIPLINE

**3a. Every factual claim has a source**
Scan for any sentence stating a fact about a party, child, or event.
Flag any fact without attribution. → 🔴 CRITICAL if the unsourced fact supports a finding.

✅ "Mother reported that Father was present at the school on [date]."
❌ "Father was present at the school on [date]." (no source)

**3b. Allegations are not stated as facts**
Flag any sentence that:
- States an unverified claim as established fact
- Converts a parent's allegation into a neutral declarative statement
- Removes attribution from a disputed claim

✅ "Mother alleged that Father was intoxicated during the exchange."
❌ "Father was intoxicated during the exchange."

**3c. Documented facts distinguished from reported statements**
When something is stated as documented, the source of documentation must be clear.
✅ "School records from [school] for [year] reflect that [child's initials] was absent [X] days."
❌ "The child had significant absences." (no source, no specifics)

**3d. Inference labeled as inference**
✅ "The GAL notes that this pattern is consistent with…"
✅ "This finding suggests…"
❌ "This proves that Father is…" / "It is clear that Mother…"

**3e. Child statements attributed to the child**
✅ "The child disclosed during the GAL interview on [date]…"
❌ "The child was harmed." (stated as fact without basis)

---

### M4 — NEUTRALITY AND LANGUAGE REVIEW

**4a. Flag advocacy language**
- "clearly," "obviously," "undeniably," "it is evident that"
- "egregious," "alarming," "deeply concerning" (without specific basis)
- "toxic," "abusive," "narcissistic," "manipulative" (labels without behavioral basis)
- Any language that sounds like a closing argument rather than a finding

**4b. Flag editorializing without behavioral basis**
Behavioral specificity standard — describe conduct, not character.
❌ "Father demonstrated a complete lack of empathy."
✅ "When asked about the children's distress during exchanges, Father stated [specific
quote]. He did not acknowledge any impact of the exchange pattern on the children."

**4c. Flag parent-favoring framing**
- Does the document describe one parent consistently favorably and the other consistently
  negatively without factual basis?
- Are negative findings about one parent documented with specifics while the other's are vague?
- Flag asymmetric treatment.

**4d. Flag inappropriate certainty**
When evidence is contested or incomplete, language must reflect that.
❌ "The GAL finds that Father abused the child."
✅ "Mother alleged that Father engaged in [conduct]. The GAL was unable to independently
corroborate or refute this allegation based on the investigation to date."

**4e. Flag emotional language**
GAL documents should not be emotionally charged. Flag:
- Language expressing the GAL's personal feelings
- Descriptions that are sympathetic rather than factual
- Any statement that reads as empathy rather than observation

---

### M5 — SCOPE COMPLIANCE

**5a. Recommendations within scope**
Every recommendation must fall within an issue the GAL was appointed to address.
Flag any recommendation addressing an issue not in the Order of Appointment.

**5b. No legal rulings**
❌ "Father is in contempt of the court order."
✅ "Father did not comply with [specific provision] of the [Order] on [date]."

❌ "This constitutes domestic violence under Florida law."
✅ "Mother alleged [specific conduct]. Law enforcement was contacted on [date]."

**5c. No expert opinions beyond GAL role**
The GAL is not a mental health expert or forensic evaluator.
❌ "Father has narcissistic personality disorder."
✅ "The forensic evaluator's report identified [clinical finding]."
✅ "The GAL observed [specific behaviors] and recommends a forensic psychological
evaluation to assess [specific concerns]."

**5d. Companion case scope**
Verify the GAL is not overreaching into findings or recommendations belonging
to companion proceedings (DCF, DV injunction, criminal).

---

### M6 — COMPLETENESS AND GAP COVERAGE

**6a. Required elements present**
See `references/document-specific-requirements.md` for minimum required content
for each document type.
For evaluation orders: verify all mandatory clauses per `references/evaluation-order-key-clauses.md`.

**6b. Contrary evidence addressed**
Flag any significant contrary account that is not addressed in the document.
A court that finds an unaddressed contrary account may question investigation completeness.

**6c. Acknowledged limitations**
Interim reports must explicitly acknowledge their own limitations.
Final reports must acknowledge remaining limitations (e.g., non-responsive sources).

**6d. All identified collaterals accounted for**
Every person the GAL communicated with must appear somewhere in the document.
Every person in the phone tag list must appear in the investigation section.

**7b. Certificate of Service**
- Present on all court filings
- Lists all counsel with correct email addresses
- Filing mechanism stated

**7c. Signature block**
- Correct address: 2400 E. Commercial Blvd., Suite 826, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33308
- Correct phone: (954) 981-2200
- GAL's name spelled correctly: Angela R. Neave, MS, Esq.

*"This Report does not include everything the GAL has learned from her investigation.
This Report is confidential. Neither this Report nor its contents shall be shared with the child."*

**7e. Evaluation orders — forensic specification**
Flag any evaluation order using "psychological evaluation" without "forensic." → 🔴 CRITICAL
Flag any evaluation order missing the collateral review clause. → 🔴 CRITICAL

### M8 — CROSS-EXAMINATION STRESS TEST

**8a. Can every recommendation be traced to a named finding?**
For each recommendation: is there a corresponding finding that supports it?
If not → flag as unsupported. → 🔴 CRITICAL

**8b. Can every finding withstand "how do you know that?"**
Is the source identified? Is it firsthand? Is it corroborated?
If the source is a single party's statement, is that stated explicitly?

**8c. Are there conclusions that could be challenged as speculation?**
Flag statements inferring motive, intent, or internal state without behavioral basis.
❌ "Mother is attempting to alienate the children from Father."
✅ "Mother has engaged in the following documented conduct that has affected the
children's relationship with Father: [specific list]."

**8d. Are there vague recommendations that couldn't be enforced?**
Flag any recommendation that:
- Does not name who is responsible
- Does not specify a timeline
- Does not specify a provider or setting
- Could not be drafted into an enforceable order paragraph as written

**8e. Would a new judge understand this document without prior case knowledge?**
Read as a cold reader. Flag any reference assuming knowledge not stated in the document.

**8f. Are there statements the GAL cannot personally verify?**
Flag any first-person statement ("I observed," "I noted") describing something the
GAL did not directly witness — use attribution instead.

## Critical Flag Reference — Quick Scan

Always scan for these first. Any one is an automatic 🔴 CRITICAL:

| Red Flag | Why Critical |
|----------|-------------|
| Child's full name in court filing body text | Privacy / convention violation |
| Allegation stated as established fact | Factual integrity failure |
| Recommendation with no corresponding finding | Unsupported conclusion |
| "Forensic" omitted from psych eval order | Wrong type of evaluation ordered |
| Collateral review clause missing from eval order | Evaluator scope inadequate |
| Outdated address / Plantation / Solaris Legal | Wrong firm contact information |
| Phone number other than (954) 981-2200 | Wrong firm contact information |
| Party's role inconsistent mid-document | Confusing / potentially incorrect |
| Date that precedes appointment or contradicts timeline | Factual error |
| GAL diagnosing or making clinical determination | Exceeds scope |
| Finding stated without any source attribution | Cannot be defended |
| Prior report recommendations contradicted without explanation | Credibility issue |
| Confidentiality footer missing from report | Required element absent |
