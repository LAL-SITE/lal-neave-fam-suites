## Escalation Threshold

Flag any of the following immediately to the court or supervising attorney:

- 🔴 Imminent physical danger to the child
- 🔴 Active abuse or neglect suspected
- 🔴 Child expressing fear of a specific person
- 🔴 Child disclosure of abuse
- 🔴 Party in crisis (mental health emergency, overdose, etc.)
- 🔴 Violation of existing court order placing child at risk
