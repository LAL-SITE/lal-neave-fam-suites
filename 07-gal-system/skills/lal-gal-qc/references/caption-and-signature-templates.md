## Standard Court Caption

```

IN RE: THE MATTER OF:

        Petitioner,

and

        Respondent.
```

## Report Title Block

```
[ORDINAL — e.g., FIRST / SECOND / THIRD / FOURTH] [INTERIM / FINAL]
REPORT OF THE GUARDIAN AD LITEM*

COMES NOW, [GAL FULL NAME, CREDENTIALS], on [his/her/their] own behalf as
Guardian Ad Litem ("GAL") in the above styled cause and who submits this
[his/her/their] [ordinal] Report of the Guardian Ad Litem and in support
thereof states the following:

*This Report does not include everything the GAL has learned from [his/her/their]
investigation. This Report is confidential. Neither this Report nor its contents
shall be shared with the child.
```

## Child Initials Protocol

> ⚠️ REMINDER: Use the child's initials only in the body of the report.
> Do not use the child's full name except in the caption and the appointment paragraph.
> Format: First initial + Last initial (e.g., "A.B.")
> If multiple children: "A.B." and "C.B." — or as designated by local practice.
